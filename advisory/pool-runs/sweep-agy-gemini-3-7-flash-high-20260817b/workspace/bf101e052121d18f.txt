This design lowers the accepted @5 proposal for identity `striatum-next/passes/stalls-need-no-judgment-c` — consumed as the accepted L1 head, the ADV-001-reconciled revision — into the decision structure for the totality packet, serving RQ-71805 under the Principal's 2026-07-22 packet-order directive. It advances target state `stalls-need-no-judgment`, predicate version 5, clauses eight through eleven, together with the fixture promotion, the four standing guards the @5 evaluation bound names, and the canonical documentation D0001.C13 requires of any fully admitted capability. This version revises the candidate refused at design-review (adversarial review 71961, verdict `needs_revision`, one major finding REV-008, no other findings, no dissent). The review holds the prior totality, evidence-basis, check-binding, and documentation defects substantially closed — every disposition embodied in the predecessor is preserved here in full — and names one remaining gap: the clause-eleven standing guard leaves the era-bounded mootness direction, expressly required by predicate version 5, without mechanical coverage. REV-008's disposition is adopted in the review's own terms; the revision context below maps the finding to the revised text.

Discipline of this artifact, stated up front: it decides what should exist, why, with what interfaces, semantics, and invariants, and what was rejected. Where it fixes an order of events, that order is runtime semantics of the designed mechanisms (which record precedes which on a ledger path); it introduces no execution sequencing of the delivery and no packet decomposition — those ambiguity classes belong to later stages (D0003.C3, D0003.C4). It names no execution backend and no model (P9). It mints no term, no artifact kind, no record kind, no escalation reason code, no budget kind, no quiescence label, no gate (D0001.C6, D0001.C10, D0001.C12/N11, D0003.C2, D0003.C7). It consumes no text outside its pinned environment and pinned inputs (el:environment-pins), and none of its mechanisms' behavior is conditional on any unpinned text (D0003.C3).

## Scope: what should exist and why {#el:scope}

Seven things exist when this packet is delivered. None is new ontology; each strengthens a seam that already exists (N11):

1. **A totality postcondition on the plan-close path** — clause eight. The plan grain gains the postcondition it has never had: a close that leaves any live request wedged says so, as a typed escalation appended before its plan record — and the postcondition ranges over **every live request the close leaves**, served or not, because the accepted predicate binds on what a session leaves, not on what it happened to engage (REV-004, adopted). The why is the 2026-07-21 RQ-57950 endgame: four successive zero-run plans, each a silent dead-end out of authority/selection recovery, none visible to the @3/@4 machinery because every existing bound trips on steps that exist. Waiting and wedged were indistinguishable on the ledger, so the difference was reconstructed five times by copied-store forensics — the exact Principal-debugging burden this target state exists to remove. The escalation's record shape is source-discriminated from the @4 exhaustion class so that no evidence guard is weakened or evaded (el:evidence-bases).
2. **A typed frontier-consumption claim across the planner's recovery seam family** — clause nine. The bare-boolean "consumed" convention (return-true-emit-nothing, dozens of sites) is retired by a claim type that cannot be constructed without a diagnosis naming the claiming branch and the failed proof. The accounting is total over live requests, service included: a request left unserved at close yields the service fall-through diagnosis, so "not served" without a diagnosis is as unrepresentable as a bare consumed claim. The why: which branch consumed the frontier — or which selection rule left the request unserved — and which proof failed is precisely the content the clause-eight escalation must carry, and today it is recorded nowhere.
3. **The oscillation detector delivered under the review-force rule** — clause ten, with the pinned predicate's clause-ten text as its normative source. Content-address identity with a previously refused or rejected candidate for the same identity and pass, where the predecessor's adverse verdict recomputes binding under the consumed force interface, is livelock regardless of remaining bounds; the firing escalates under the existing budget vocabulary, carrying exactly what the clause-ten text requires — both versions and the binding verdict — in its own evidence basis, with nothing about the successful producing runs relabeled as failure (el:oscillation-detector, el:evidence-bases). The why: candidates 70757/70811/70835 were content-identical, one already personally rejected, all producing runs successful — invisible to failure-signature machinery, ended by a prompt-wording patch where the designed detector should have spoken. The force qualifier is ADV-001's adopted disposition: without it, the detector misfires on lawful regeneration — the content-preserving rebuild after a context move that this identity's own lineage exercises.
4. **A blocking-scope consultation in the quiescence classifier** — clause eleven. `awaiting_principal` is reported only on the strength of an unresolved escalation whose blocking scope lies on a live request's dependency path, evaluated by the fold's existing mootness rule (State.Blocks). The why: all four 07-21 stalls quiesced `awaiting_principal` off unrelated escalations, and an era-bounded inert escalation on a satisfied request can pin the whole graph's label today.
5. **Durable fixtures with lawful roles** — the four preserved RQ-57950 copied stores, checked into the repository as primary fixtures (Product Artifacts, one role each), with minimal derived controls, a checked-in fixture manifest, and the incident-evidence claim carried by admitted evidence with explicit content pins (el:fixture-doctrine, C15). The why: the counterexamples are perishable as env-gated /tmp state, the guards need exactly this material, and the preservation claim must be represented lawfully under D0001.C2 — by evidence artifacts pinning the bytes, never by role-merging.
6. **Four standing guards, and the advanced clause-five check bound beside them** — one guard per @5 clause, registered checks named in the delivery packet's acceptance_checks under acceptance-checks-bind@1, each replaying the wired seam over the fixtures with both firing and non-firing assertions, each verifying its fixtures against the manifest before replay; and — because C14 advances the @4 clause-five check's subject selection to consume the evidence-basis discriminator — that check at its advanced registry identity is named in the same acceptance_checks and executed over the same materialized tree, demonstrated against all three basis classes (REV-006, adopted). The why: the @2 hollow-Verified incident — satisfaction of this state must be impossible without the guards executing and passing — and D0005.C9's complete-check closure: a changed check ships only exercised.
7. **The capability's canonical documentation** — the Diátaxis explanation, reference, and how-to surfaces for everything above, as Product Artifacts in this packet's product lineage, admitted through the existing gate classes (el:documentation-surface, C16). The why is D0001.C13: a capability is fully admitted only when implementation, justification, and explanation hold simultaneously in the artifact graph; a packet that delivered these mechanisms with their explanation lagging would put the graph in the exact state that clause forbids (REV-007, adopted).

The compound effect: the ledger can finally express wedged-versus-waiting, every wedged state arrives before authority carrying its own diagnosis, and the capability that does so arrives explained. Deciding stays with the Principal; debugging leaves.

## Definitions {#el:definitions}

These definitions fix the semantic ground the mechanisms and clauses bind to. Each names an existing concept or a total function of existing ledger facts; none introduces a stored notion of its own.

- **Session.** One Driver planning invocation: it reads the store, performs selection and recovery for the live requests it serves, prepares zero or more runs, and closes by appending its plan record. The postcondition binds at this grain because the plan record is the single artifact of a session's close — the last lawful moment at which silence can be refused. The close's postcondition evaluation ranges over every live request in the store (C1), not only the served ones.
- **Live request.** A request whose close is unwritten — neither satisfied nor cancelled.
- **Unmet executable conjunct set** — `unmet(R)`. The conjuncts of request R's target predicate that are unmet and currently executable, as the fold already computes them.
- **Frontier.** For a request R at a session: `unmet(R)` together with the session's lawful continuations for advancing any member — preparing runs, recording scheduling deferrals, or routing through recovery. The frontier is **consumed** when selection and recovery terminate for R with no continuation produced.
- **Stall configuration.** Request R is in stall configuration at a session's close iff all five hold on the state that close would produce (post-selection, pre-append — the session's own prepared runs and deferrals count):
  - (a) `unmet(R) ≠ ∅`;
  - (b) the close leaves zero prepared runs serving R — none prepared by this session and none standing from before;
  - (c) no open run serves R;
  - (d) no scheduling deferral scoped to R carries a retry horizon in the future;
  - (e) no unresolved escalation's blocking scope lies on R's dependency path.

  The configuration is a property of the close state and the request alone; whether the session served R does not enter it.
- **Lawful wait.** Condition (a) true with any of (c)–(e) failing: R is waiting on a run already open, a horizon already recorded, or authority already summoned on its path. A failing (b) is progress, not a wait; a failing (a) means nothing is unmet and no wait exists. The subtraction is by construction: each of (a)–(e) is a ledger fact the session already holds at close — no history heuristic, no timing, no discretion.
- **Frontier-consumption diagnosis.** The typed value returned when a recovery branch claims R's frontier: the **branch** (stable identifier of the claiming branch), the **proof** (stable identifier of the proof or precondition whose failure ended selection), and the **subject** (the request, and where narrower the conjunct or element the proof was evaluated against). Branch and proof are mandatory and drawn from identifier sets fixed at the seam; the diagnosis is non-empty by shape.
- **Service fall-through diagnosis.** The same diagnosis type, produced for a live request the session's close leaves unserved: its branch identifier names the service-selection seam — the seam that fixed the session's served set — and its proof identifier names the selection rule under which the request was left unserved. It exists so the accounting of C3 is total over live requests, not merely served ones, and so the C2 escalation for an unserved stalled request carries a diagnosis of the same guarded shape.
- **Attempt terminal record.** The terminal record of an executed attempt — a run's close with its outcome facts — the record class the @4 machinery already cites and aggregates.
- **Evidence basis.** The source-discriminated evidence a `bounds_exhausted` escalation carries: exactly one of **attempt basis** (non-empty cited set of attempt terminal records plus the aggregated failure-signature summary derived from exactly those records), **frontier basis** (empty cited-attempt set plus the frontier-consumption or service fall-through diagnosis), or **oscillation basis** (the candidate versions and binding adverse verdict clause ten requires, with the producing run cited as scope provenance only). Defined normatively in el:evidence-bases and C14.
- **Blocking scope, dependency path, on-path.** Per the fold's existing mootness rule (State.Blocks): an unresolved escalation blocks the scope it names; R's dependency path is the transitive prerequisite cone the fold already computes. An escalation is **on-path** for R when its blocking scope intersects R's dependency path. Era-bounded escalations and escalations scoped to satisfied requests recompute moot under the same rule — off-path by evaluation, never by special case.
- **Verdict force and the consumed force interface.** Per D0005.C10 as the accepted proposal restates it (proposal el:clause-ten, el:constraints): a review or acceptance verdict binds now iff its structured semantic context — target, subject/base/product context, contracts, gates and evaluator, environment pins — recomputes equal to the current one; content equality alone is insufficient. This design consumes that determination through one interface: `force(adverse verdict record v, current structured semantic context K) → {binding, forceless}`, evaluated by the review machinery's existing D0005.C10 recomputation. The rule's owning text is D0005.C10; this design reads no D0005 text, consumes the rule as the accepted proposal restates it, and holds no applicability logic of its own (P2 — one rule, its existing evaluator, this design a consumer).
- **Adverse verdict.** A verdict of `needs_revision` or `reject` from the closed verdict vocabulary (D0001.C6), at the subject pass's review or acceptance gate. The 70811 personal rejection is an acceptance-gate `reject`.
- **Content identity.** Two candidate versions are content-identical iff the artifact store assigns them the same content address. No similarity or near-duplicate notion exists anywhere in this design.
- **Oscillation configuration.** A produced candidate c of identity I and pass P for which there exists a prior candidate c′ of (I, P) with the same content address carrying an adverse verdict whose force recomputes binding under the consumed force interface.
- **Fixture manifest.** A checked-in file in the standing-guards tree listing every primary fixture's content address and every derived control's named primary and delta. Ordinary product content — a file, not a term, kind, or record admission.
- **Canonical documentation surface.** The repository's canonical Diátaxis documentation, per D0001.C13: Product Artifacts in the artifact graph — never commentary outside it — organized by the Diátaxis modes (tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation) where a mode has subject matter. This design consumes the concept as D0001.C13 admits it and fixes content obligations for this capability (el:documentation-surface); it mints no documentation ontology.

## Mechanism: plan-grain totality postcondition {#el:totality-postcondition}

Delivers clause eight. The plan-write path — the single seam through which every session closes — gains a postcondition evaluated over every live request, so that a close that leaves any request wedged is unwritable without its escalation.

### Interface {#el:totality-interface}

Evaluated on the plan-close path, after selection and recovery have completed and before the plan record is appended. Contract, per **live request R in the store at close — served by this session or not** (REV-004, adopted):

- compute stall configuration (a)–(e) over the close state;
- if R is in stall configuration: append the stall escalation for R;
- after all live requests are evaluated: append the plan record.

The stall escalation's form: reason code `bounds_exhausted`; scope the stalled request R (which is also its blocking scope); `budget_kind` the member of the closed vocabulary that names attempt budgets — the member the @4 attempt-budget escalations already carry, read here at its degenerate case: a frontier admitting zero further attempts is an attempt budget exhausted at zero. Its evidence is the frontier basis (el:evidence-bases): the diagnosis carried verbatim over an empty cited-attempt set — the frontier-consumption diagnosis where this session's recovery seam consumed R's frontier, the service fall-through diagnosis where the close leaves R unserved — nothing executed, so nothing is cited, and no aggregated failure-signature summary exists, is claimed, or is fabricated. It enters the existing self-dedupe under that mechanism's unchanged identity; no new suppression logic.

### Semantics {#el:totality-semantics}

- **Per-request, over the whole store.** The close evaluates every live request; each stalled request receives its own escalation, each scoped to that request. A session serving one request over a store holding another live request in stall configuration appends the second request's escalation too — the postcondition is a property of what the close leaves, not of what the session engaged. Silence outside the stall configuration remains lawful — a zero-run plan whose live requests all hold lawful waits appends nothing.
- **Ordering and the loud failure direction.** Escalation appends precede the plan-record append. An interruption between the two leaves an escalation without a plan record — loud, and correct: it summons attention to a session that died mid-close. The reverse orphan — a stalled request's plan record without its escalation — is unrepresentable on the lawful path. That asymmetry is the point of the ordering.
- **Self-stabilizing closure with clause eleven.** Once appended, the stall escalation is itself an unresolved escalation whose blocking scope (the request) lies on the request's own dependency path. Two consequences: the next session over an unchanged store finds conjunct (e) failing and recomputes a lawful wait — the first escalation quiets its successors by construction, with the existing self-dedupe covering the residual re-raise class (for example, a resolution that changes nothing, after which the stall recomputes honestly); and the quiescence classifier now reports `awaiting_principal` citing an on-path escalation that carries the diagnosis. Wedged states become: stall escalation on the ledger. Waiting states become: (b)–(e) name the wait. The distinction is one ledger read.
- **Partition with @4 clause five, not satisfaction of it.** The clause-eight record is not written on budget exhaustion: nothing ran, nothing consumed a budget, and no attempt terminal records exist from which the @4 aggregated summary could be derived — a derivation from an empty set would be fabricated provenance, and this design asserts none (REV-001, adopted and preserved). The accepted predicate already separates the two evidence obligations: the @4 bound binds "an escalation written on budget exhaustion" to a summary "derived from its attempts' terminal records," while the @5 bound gives the clause-eight escalation its own guard — "the clause-eight escalation's diagnosis field is shape-guarded non-empty (clause nine)." The record shape makes the classes discriminable from the record alone (el:evidence-bases); the @4 guard keeps its full, unweakened force over its own subject class; guard nine owns the stall class. Neither obligation is asserted away; no clause is amended.
- **Disjointness from @4 clause six and from clause ten.** The deterministic-failure short-circuit requires attempts with terminal failure signatures; the stall configuration requires zero prepared runs and cites no attempts. The oscillation firing requires a produced candidate at admission and, once fired, its escalation is on-path, so the stall configuration recomputes a lawful wait. The triggers are pairwise disjoint; no configuration double-fires.
- **Cost of the full range, bounded.** Evaluating (a)–(e) for every live request is bounded work over facts the session already holds or the fold already computes: `unmet(R)` is the fold's per-request computation, (b)–(d) are indexed ledger facts of the close state, and (e) is the fold's State.Blocks evaluation. No new store read class is introduced; the range widens, the fact set does not.
- **Honest bound, restated without the withdrawn narrowing.** The postcondition binds at plan close and therefore binds only when a session closes: a session that never runs writes no plan record and this postcondition binds nothing — that sessions occur at all remains the wake machinery's standing duty, unchanged here. Within any close, no narrowing remains: every live request the close leaves is evaluated (REV-004). The 07-21 counterexamples were served-and-stalled; the unserved-stall direction is covered by the same construction and witnessed by guard eight's unserved-stall control.

### Invariants {#el:totality-invariants}

- **Exactness.** A session's close appends stall escalations for exactly the live requests in stall configuration — no fewer (no silent wedge, served or unserved) and no more (lawful waits and progress never escalate; precision is load-bearing).
- **Classify-and-append only.** The postcondition prepares no work, resolves nothing, defers nothing, serves nothing it would not otherwise have served, and never suppresses an escalation the configuration demands.
- **Determinism.** The postcondition is a total, deterministic function of the close state; replay recomputes it identically (pinned D0002.C2).

## Mechanism: typed frontier consumption {#el:frontier-claim}

Delivers clause nine. The recovery seam family's reporting convention is replaced by a type that makes the bare claim unrepresentable — and the accounting is total over live requests, service included.

### Interface {#el:frontier-interface}

The frontier claim type has exactly two constructors: **pass** (the branch did not claim the frontier) and **consumed**, whose only constructor requires a well-formed diagnosis. Every branch in the planner's recovery seam family returns this type. There is no diagnosis-free consumed value; the retired convention is not deprecated but unexpressible.

### Semantics {#el:frontier-semantics}

- **Branches keep their behavior.** Clause nine retires a reporting convention, not the recovery branches: selection behavior is unchanged; each branch gains only the obligation to say which proof failed when it claims.
- **Total accounting over served requests.** Every path that ends selection for a served request with nothing prepared yields a diagnosis: the claiming branch's, or — where selection falls through without an explicit claim — the seam's terminal fall-through diagnosis, whose branch identifier names the fall-through and whose proof identifier names the selection proof that yielded no continuation.
- **Total accounting over unserved requests.** A live request the close leaves unserved yields the service fall-through diagnosis (el:definitions): branch the service-selection seam, proof the selection rule that left it unserved. "Not served" was itself a return-true-emit-nothing convention — the deepest one — and it is retired with the rest: at close, no live request lacks either a continuation, a lawful wait, or a diagnosis. The postcondition therefore always has a diagnosis to carry for any stalled request, and guard nine's non-emptiness is decidable unconditionally over the full C1 range.
- **Consumption by the escalation.** The clause-eight escalation carries the diagnosis verbatim. The diagnosis is a value threaded through the seam and a field of an ordinary ledger escalation — never a record type of its own (D0003.C7).

### Invariants {#el:frontier-invariants}

- **Unrepresentability.** A consumed claim without a diagnosis does not construct; an unserved live request without a service fall-through diagnosis does not close.
- **Stable identifiers.** Branch and proof identifiers — the service-selection seam's included — are fixed constants of the seam; they appear in escalation records and dedupe identities, so renaming one is a semantic change to the seam, not a cosmetic edit.
- **Structure over prose.** The guarded fields are structured identifiers; human-readable detail may accompany them but never substitutes for them.

## Evidence bases and guard subjects {#el:evidence-bases}

Fixes the source-discriminated record shape and guard behavior for every escalation carrying reason code `bounds_exhausted` — the REV-001 disposition preserved, extended by REV-005's: the clause-ten firing carries its own basis, and the attempt basis is restricted to actual attempt-terminal failure signatures. This section decides record shape and guard subjects; it amends no accepted clause.

**The three bases.** Every `bounds_exhausted` escalation carries exactly one evidence basis, fixed by its writing trigger and discriminable from the record alone:

- **Attempt basis** — trigger: budget exhaustion or provable-repetition short-circuit over executed attempts (@4 clauses five and six). Content: a non-empty cited set of attempt terminal records, and the aggregated failure-signature summary — distinct signatures with counts — derived from exactly those cited records, per the @4 bound. Restriction (REV-005, adopted): every cited record is the terminal record of an attempt that closed in failure, and every summarized signature is an actual terminal failure signature. No successful run's record grounds this basis; no production fact, content address, or verdict citation is presented as a failure signature.
- **Frontier basis** — trigger: the C1 stall configuration. Content: an empty cited-attempt set (nothing executed; nothing may be cited) and the diagnosis carried verbatim — frontier-consumption or service fall-through (C2, C3). No aggregated summary is present or claimed.
- **Oscillation basis** — trigger: the C5 oscillation firing. Content: exactly what the accepted clause-ten text obliges the escalation to carry — the new candidate's version, every content-identical predecessor version, and the binding adverse verdict record(s) — together with a citation of the new candidate's producing run as **scope provenance only**: it establishes which request the escalation scopes to, and it is expressly not a failure citation. This basis has no summary field and no failure-signature notion; the producing runs succeeded, and this record says so by carrying no failure evidence at all (REV-005, adopted).

**Constructor refusals (shape-level, at the escalation writer).** A `bounds_exhausted` escalation does not construct with: no basis; more than one basis; an attempt basis whose citation set is empty, whose summary is empty, or whose cited records include any record that is not an attempt terminal failure record; a frontier basis whose citation set is non-empty or whose diagnosis is absent or ill-formed; an oscillation basis whose new-candidate version is absent, whose predecessor version set is empty, or whose verdict citation is absent or cites a verdict that is not adverse. Hollow or mislabeled evidence is unwritable in every class.

**Guard subjects partition three ways.** The @4 clause-five guard binds exactly attempt-basis records — its accepted subject term is already trigger-scoped: "an escalation written on budget exhaustion carries a non-empty aggregated failure-signature summary derived from its attempts' terminal records." Its obligations over that class are unchanged and un-weakened. Guard nine binds exactly frontier-basis records. Guard ten binds the oscillation-basis form of C5 firings. The constructor refusal makes the three obligations jointly total over `bounds_exhausted` records: every record carries exactly one basis, each basis has exactly one obligation owner, no record evades all, and no record answers to another class's obligation.

**The reconciliation is located in accepted text, not manufactured here.** Three sentences of the pinned predicate assign three evidence contents to three firing classes: the @4 bound binds escalations written on budget exhaustion to a summary "derived from its attempts' terminal records"; the @5 bound's clause-nine guard binds the clause-eight escalation to a non-empty diagnosis; and the clause-ten text binds the oscillation escalation to "carrying both versions" (the accepted proposal adding "and the predecessor verdict that binds"). Read as one coherent accepted text — and it is one text, predicate version 5 — the three evidence obligations bind disjoint trigger classes, and the record shape makes each class discriminable so each obligation can be enforced without evasion or cross-answering. Before clauses eight and ten existed, reason-code and trigger extensionally coincided, so a code-keyed subject selection was harmlessly equal to the accepted term; the new clauses make the classes distinguishable, and the selection must say which — per the bounds' own words. Nothing requires or receives amendment.

**Registered-check consequence.** The registered clause-five check's subject selection consumes the discriminator to keep tracking its accepted class — exactly attempt-basis records — under the check registry's own versioning discipline, with its assertions unchanged. The design admits no state in which a frontier-basis or oscillation-basis record is evaluated under the attempt-basis obligation, and no state in which the advanced selection ships unexercised: the discriminator, the escalation writer, and the subject selection are one designed invariant of one record shape, and this packet's own verification executes the advanced check over the same materialized tree as the guards, against all three basis classes (C10; REV-006, adopted).

## Mechanism: oscillation detection under the review-force rule {#el:oscillation-detector}

Delivers clause ten: the oscillation detector, composed with the review-force rule and placed at the admission seam. Its normative source is the pinned @5 predicate's clause-ten text — "a new candidate whose content hash matches a previously refused or rejected candidate for the same identity and pass is livelock regardless of remaining bounds, and escalates carrying both versions" — read, as the accepted proposal directs for this packet, within the standing decision records and never in suspension of them. No fuller RFC 0006 text is consumed by this design, and no behavior of this mechanism is conditional on one (D0003.C3).

### Interface {#el:oscillation-interface}

At the admission seam where a produced candidate of identity I and pass P enters and would open its review ladder, the Driver evaluates the oscillation configuration:

- collect the prior candidates of (I, P) whose content address equals the new candidate's — the address the store already assigns at admission; no new hash notion;
- for each such predecessor carrying an adverse verdict, evaluate the consumed force interface (el:definitions) — the review machinery's existing D0005.C10 recomputation;
- if any predecessor's adverse verdict recomputes binding: livelock. Append one escalation — reason code `bounds_exhausted`; scope the request the producing run served (which is its blocking scope); `budget_kind` the attempt-budget member, the ladder's bounded revision attempts being the budget the proof forecloses; evidence the oscillation basis (el:evidence-bases): the new candidate's version, every content-identical predecessor version, and the binding adverse verdict record(s), with the producing run cited as scope provenance only — no failure signature claimed, no summary present, no successful-run fact relabeled (REV-005, adopted). Records citing records: adjudication reads the escalation and its causal records alone. The review ladder does not open;
- otherwise: admit exactly as today, with no escalation and no record of the non-firing.

### Semantics {#el:oscillation-semantics}

- **Two tests, each necessary, together sufficient.** Content-address equality establishes that the same work is re-entering; the force recomputation establishes that its refusal still speaks. A detector holding only the first misfires on lawful regeneration — the pin-only rebuild after a context move, hash-identical to a rejected predecessor, which this identity's own lineage exercises — and each misfire manufactures a judgment-requiring escalation, the artifact this target state exists to retire.
- **Regardless of remaining bounds.** Remaining revision budget is not consulted for firing and not extended by it. Riding `bounds_exhausted` while budget remains is not a contradiction but the @4 pattern: the accepted predicate already escalates provable repetition without burning the remainder ("a third dispatch after two identical failure signatures is refused in favor of immediate escalation"). The budget-kind field names the budget the proof forecloses — the ladder's attempt budget — not a claim that its count reached zero. Bounds are touched in one direction only: escalating earlier.
- **Run success is no defense — and is not relabeled.** The trigger is a property of product content and standing verdicts, not of run outcomes; the 70757/70811/70835 loop ran on clean closes. The record honors that: its evidence is versions and the binding verdict, its producing-run citation is scope provenance, and nothing in it presents success as failure. What forecloses the ladder is the standing adverse verdict on identical content, and that is exactly — and only — what the record carries (REV-005, adopted).
- **Work is never discarded.** The produced candidate version remains recorded in the store (pinned D0002.C4); what the firing refuses is the ladder, not the evidence.
- **One firing per admission.** A single escalation cites the full content-identical match set; re-raise across admissions is governed by the existing self-dedupe like every escalation.
- **Complementarity, no double fire.** @4 clause six binds to identical failure signatures of erroring attempts; this detector binds to content identity of successfully produced candidates — disjoint triggers, disjoint bases. With C1: once fired, this escalation is on-path for the served request, so the stall configuration recomputes a lawful wait at the next plan close; the two mechanisms compose without double-firing.
- **Resolution is authority's alone.** The escalation is terminal for the mechanism. No cancellation, redirection, or rebuild is initiated by it — pre-scripting any of those is forbidden (Principal rule, 2026-07-06).
- **Honest bound.** Trivially varied resubmission defeats exact content identity by construction. That evasion class is out of scope: bounded budgets still exhaust under the @4 clauses, and the exact-repeat class is the one the predicate designed against and the incident exhibited.

### Authorization {#el:oscillation-authorization}

The chain, entirely within pinned texts and pinned inputs: the pinned @5 predicate mandates delivering the detector with the clause-ten trigger semantics and names the escalation's required content ("carrying both versions"); the accepted proposal — the L1 head this design lowers, revised under adversarial review 71844 and accepted with ADV-001's disposition adopted — mandates the force qualifier, adds the binding-verdict content, and expressly holds that the predicate is read within the standing decision records; the closed vocabulary (pinned D0001.C6/C10) supplies the reason code, with clause eight of the same predicate demonstrating the pattern — a new stall class rides `bounds_exhausted` with budget kind and scope rather than minting. The oscillation basis is record shape under that closed vocabulary, its required content the clause-ten text's own. Every element of C5/C6 is therefore authorized by an accepted text this design lawfully reads; no element waits on a text it cannot.

### Invariants {#el:oscillation-invariants}

- **Placement.** Detection is a Driver admission-seam function — single-writer territory with the full lineage in hand (pinned D0002.C4/C5); it is never delegated to untrusted execution and never spends a review to discover doomed content.
- **No parallel applicability.** The detector holds no freshness or context logic of its own; verdict force comes from the one existing recomputation through the consumed interface (P2 — one rule, two consumers).
- **Both directions bind.** Firing on a binding predecessor verdict and admitting on a forceless one are equally load-bearing; the guard exercises both.
- **Evidence honesty.** The firing cites no failure: production facts remain production facts, verdicts remain verdicts, and the record class that carries failure signatures (the attempt basis) is never inhabited by this mechanism.
- **Unconditional behavior.** The mechanism's behavior is a total function of the store at the admission seam: lineage addresses, adverse verdict records, and the force interface's result. No branch consults any text outside the pinned environment.

## Mechanism: blocking-scope-aware quiescence {#el:quiescence-classifier}

Delivers clause eleven. The quiescence classifier consults the mootness rule the fold already evaluates.

### Interface {#el:quiescence-interface}

A quiescent session reports `awaiting_principal` iff there exists an unresolved escalation whose blocking scope lies on a live request's dependency path — evaluated by consuming the fold's State.Blocks evaluation, never by re-deriving it. Absent such an escalation, classification proceeds under the existing label set without `awaiting_principal`; no new label is minted.

### Semantics {#el:quiescence-semantics}

- **Moot escalations stop pinning.** Off-path escalations, era-bounded escalations, and escalations scoped to satisfied requests recompute moot under the fold's rule and never set the label. The retired counterexamples are the four 07-21 misclassifications and the predicate's era-bounded case — the inert escalation on a satisfied request that today pins the whole graph's label; guard eleven witnesses every mootness direction mechanically, the era-bound arm in isolation (C9, C10; REV-008, adopted).
- **The label becomes evidence-backed.** With clause eight in force, a wedged request carries an on-path stall escalation, so `awaiting_principal` is reported exactly when the ledger holds the escalation that justifies it — and that escalation carries the diagnosis. The label stops being a default and starts being a citation.
- **Visibility is not classification.** Unresolved off-path escalations remain fully visible through the existing derived status surface with its warnings-versus-notices triage (pinned D0002.C10); the classifier decides the quiescence label, never what is shown.
- **The starvation warning stands unamended.** Its requirement that planned runs exist is unchanged; its zero-run blind spot is covered by clause eight's escalation, not by a second overlapping detector.

### Invariants {#el:quiescence-invariants}

- **Single source.** Blockedness has one definition — the fold's — with the fold, the clause-eight conjunct (e), and this classifier as its consumers. A parallel notion would be the P2 shadow this repository forbids.
- **Read-only and derived.** The classifier appends nothing and remains recomputable from the store (pinned D0002.C2).

## Fixture doctrine {#el:fixture-doctrine}

Delivers the fixture promotion the @5 evaluation bound names, with roles lawful under D0001.C2 (REV-002, adopted and preserved).

- **One role per artifact.** The four preserved RQ-57950 copied stores enter the repository as Product Artifacts in exactly one role: primary fixtures, the standing guards' replay material. The once-refused dual-role doctrine stays withdrawn; no checked-in store is ontologically "evidence."
- **Primaries.** Bytes as captured, except where credential-freedom requires a scrub, asserted at admission: values, never credentials, may enter the tree; a store that cannot satisfy that is scrubbed under a documented delta or refused, never checked in silently. One directory per store under the standing-guards tree, readable repository-relative with no environment gate, no /tmp path, and no network.
- **The incident-evidence claim rides admitted evidence, not the fixtures.** The world-authored capture of the four stores and their entry into this repository are recorded by exogenous change record(s) — the admitted evidence kind (D0003.C2) that this target state's own `evidence_admitted` v0 conjunct names — carrying explicit content pins for every primary (the checked-in tree's content address), capture provenance (request RQ-57950, the 2026-07-21 window, the source store), and the documented delta for any scrub. Record grain follows the observation pass's standing convention; this design fixes the content obligations, not the record count. Evidence artifacts carry evidence invalidation semantics — append-only history, never invalidated by later tree motion; fixtures carry product semantics — product lineage and staleness like any Product Artifact. The roles never merge (D0001.C2).
- **Fixture manifest.** A checked-in manifest in the standing-guards tree lists every primary's content address and every derived control's named primary and delta. Each guard verifies the fixtures it consumes against the manifest before replay — repository-only, no ledger read in guards; manifest-to-evidence-record correspondence is verified on the graph side by the machinery that already verifies such pins.
- **Never-edit rule, mechanical.** Primaries are never edited to make a guard pass — and the rule has teeth rather than sentiment: an edit changes the primary's content address and fails loudly against both the manifest and the evidence record's pins. A configuration a primary does not exhibit is supplied by a derived control, never by a primary edit.
- **Derived controls.** A derived control is a minimal, documented, single-delta mutation of a named primary, checked in beside it and listed in the manifest, existing to exercise a direction the primaries cannot. The minimum set: a **lawful-wait control** (an open-run fact added, under which the postcondition must stay silent); a **context-move control** (a predecessor adverse verdict's structured semantic context moved, under which the oscillation detector must not fire); an **on-path control** (an unresolved on-path escalation present, under which `awaiting_principal` must be reported); an **era-bound control** (the on-path control's added escalation carrying additionally an era bound on which the fold's State.Blocks evaluation excludes it — otherwise on-path, its blocking scope on a live request's dependency path, moot solely by the era-bound arm, under which `awaiting_principal` must be withheld; the on-path/era-bound pair differs in exactly that bound, so together they isolate the arm — the REV-008 direction); a **satisfied-request control** (an unresolved era-bounded inert escalation whose blocking scope lies on a satisfied request's dependency path, in a store still holding live requests — the pinned predicate's own clause-eleven counterexample, under which `awaiting_principal` must likewise be withheld); an **unserved-stall control** (a live request in stall configuration left outside the session's served set, under which the C1 escalation must fire — the REV-004 counterexample); and an **attempt-basis exemplar control** (a lawful @4-class attempt-budget escalation configuration with cited attempt terminal failure records, material for the advanced clause-five check's positive direction under C10 — the REV-006 material).
- **Repository-only execution.** Guards and the advanced clause-five check run from the repository alone; the fixture set is sufficient for every assertion they make. Where a primary exhibits a needed configuration (the four zero-run stalls; the preserved oscillation lineage where present), the guard replays it; where it does not, the derived controls carry the direction.

## Standing guards {#el:standing-guards}

Delivers the @5 evaluation bound: one standing guard per clause, with a common doctrine forced by the @2 hollow-Verified incident — and, per REV-006, the advanced clause-five check bound into the same verification.

**Common doctrine.** Each guard replays the wired seam over fixtures — it exercises the mechanism where it lives, not an extracted function beside it. Each guard begins by verifying its consumed fixtures' content addresses against the checked-in manifest. Each guard contains at least one firing and one non-firing assertion: a guard that cannot observe its mechanism's absence is vacuously satisfiable and is refused at design. - **Guard eight — frontier totality.** Over each primary fixture: run a planning session; assert the typed escalation is appended for the stalled request with its full C2 form — reason `bounds_exhausted`, scope the request, the attempt-budget kind, frontier basis: empty cited-attempt set, diagnosis present, no aggregated summary populated — and that its append precedes the plan record's; assert the plan record exists — the escalation joins the plan record, never replaces it. Over the **unserved-stall control**: assert the escalation is appended for the stalled request the session never served, carrying the service fall-through diagnosis — the postcondition's full range witnessed (REV-004). Over the lawful-wait control: assert no escalation is appended. Firing, unserved firing, and non-firing all asserted.
- **Guard nine — diagnosis shape and basis discipline.** Over every escalation produced in guard eight's replays: assert the diagnosis is shape-valid — branch and proof present, non-empty, stable identifiers, frontier-consumption and service fall-through cases both — and the frontier basis well-formed. Additionally assert the writer's construction-time refusals (el:evidence-bases) across all three bases: an empty or ill-formed diagnosis, a basis-less record, a multi-basis record, a frontier basis with a non-empty citation set, an attempt basis citing a non-failure record, and an oscillation basis missing a version or binding verdict are each refused. The non-firing direction is the writer's refusal.
- **Guard ten — oscillation, both directions.** Firing: replay admission of a content-identical candidate whose predecessor adverse verdict recomputes binding — from the preserved 70757/70811/70835 lineage where a primary exhibits it, else from the fixture doctrine's controls — and assert the escalation's full C5 form: reason code, scope, attempt-budget kind, oscillation basis carrying the new version, every content-identical predecessor version, and the binding verdict citation, with the producing run cited as scope provenance and no summary or failure-signature field present — and that no review ladder opens. Non-firing: over the context-move control, assert admission proceeds with no escalation. Both directions are the predicate's own text.
- **Guard eleven — quiescence scope, every mootness direction (REV-008, adopted).** Negative, off-path: over the primaries as captured — whose unresolved escalations are all off-path, which is the misclassification evidence itself — assert classification does not report `awaiting_principal`. Negative, era-bounded: over the era-bound control — whose escalation is otherwise on a live request's dependency path and excluded solely by the fold's era-bound evaluation — assert `awaiting_principal` is withheld. Because that control differs from the on-path control in exactly the era bound, the pair isolates the era-bound arm: a classifier that consults path and liveness but ignores era bounds passes the off-path and positive assertions and fails this one. Negative, satisfied-request: over the satisfied-request control — the predicate's own counterexample, an era-bounded inert escalation on a satisfied request in a store still holding live requests — assert `awaiting_principal` is withheld. Positive control: over the on-path control, or equivalently over a primary's post-guard-eight state (whose stall escalation is on-path), assert `awaiting_principal` is reported. The positive control exists so the guard cannot be satisfied by a classifier that never reports the label at all — and it witnesses the clause-eight/clause-eleven closure end to end.

**The advanced clause-five check in this packet (REV-006, adopted).** The @4 clause-five registered check — the `stalls-guards` member whose accepted obligation is the aggregated failure-signature summary of escalations written on budget exhaustion — advances under the check registry's versioning discipline to the subject selection that consumes the C14 discriminator, with its assertions unchanged. A broken or unchanged selector fails this run in one direction or the other: failing to select the exemplar fails the positive direction; selecting a frontier-basis or oscillation-basis record fails the exclusion direction. The changed check cannot ship unexercised (D0005.C9's complete-check closure, binding here as el:environment-pins records).

## Tradeoffs {#el:tradeoffs}

- **In-session postcondition over an external watcher.** The session holds every fact the subtraction needs at the exact moment silence would otherwise be written; a watcher adds a component, a race, and a timing heuristic. Cost accepted: every plan close pays the evaluation — bounded work over facts already in hand.
- **Full live-request range over served-only range.** The predicate binds on what a close leaves, so the postcondition evaluates every live request, not only the engaged ones. Cost accepted: closes over stores with many live requests evaluate (a)–(e) for each — bounded, because the facts are the fold's existing per-request computations and indexed close-state facts; no new read class. Bought: the unserved silent wedge is unwritable, and totality is the predicate's own, not a service-scoped approximation of it (REV-004).
- **`bounds_exhausted` reuse over new reason codes — for both clause eight and clause ten.** Forced for clause eight by the closed vocabulary and the predicate's own text; decided for clause ten because the code is the budget-family member the pinned vocabulary names, the firing forecloses a real bounded budget by proof, and the escalate-earlier direction is the @4 pattern. Cost accepted: the budget-kind sense stretches — degenerate zero for the stall case, foreclosed-by-proof for oscillation; mitigated because each record's evidence basis carries the real content and the escalation stays authority-shaped. The alternative buys labels at the price of an RFC 0001 amendment.
- **Three evidence bases under one code over per-trigger codes.** Cost accepted: a three-way discriminator discipline at the escalation writer. Mitigated by shape-level constructor refusals and jointly-total guard subjects — every record carries exactly one basis, each basis has one obligation owner, no record evades all or answers to the wrong one. Per-trigger codes would amend the closed vocabulary for distinctions the record shape already carries.
- **An oscillation basis over attempt-basis placement.** The refused predecessor placed C5 firings in the attempt basis, relabeling the shared content address as an aggregated signature of successful runs — which could not satisfy the attempt basis's own derivation invariant without fabricated provenance (REV-005). Cost accepted: a third case at the writer and in guard subjects. Bought: evidence honesty — the attempt basis stays restricted to actual attempt-terminal failure signatures, the @4 guard's subject class stays pure, and the oscillation record's required content is exactly the clause-ten text's.
- **The pinned predicate text as the detector's normative source over importing RFC 0006's fuller text.** Cost accepted: if the fuller text carries more — a dedicated code, additional conditions — this packet does not deliver the surplus. Mitigated: the predicate governs for this packet (the accepted proposal's own rule), the delivered semantics are exactly the predicate's, and future environment movement is ordinary staleness handled by the standing machinery. The alternatives are an unpinned read (hermeticity violation) or a deferral (the conditional design D0003.C3 forbids and review 71896 refused).
- **Escalation-before-plan ordering.** Buys the guarantee that the silent orphan is unrepresentable. Cost accepted: an interruption can leave an escalation without its plan record — the loud orphan, which summons exactly the attention a mid-close death deserves.
- **Total type change over incremental annotation.** Dozens of sites change at once so that no branch can claim silently ever again; a convention half-retired is the convention kept. Cost accepted: one-time churn across the seam family, purchased for compile-grain permanence.
- **Terminal fall-through diagnoses over mandatory explicit claims only.** Totality of accounting means the postcondition never lacks a diagnosis — the service fall-through case included — at the cost that a fall-through diagnosis is coarser than a branch's. Accepted: an honest "selection fell through at proof X" or "left unserved under rule Y" still names the seam and still beats silence; branch-grain precision grows wherever branches claim explicitly.
- **Detection at admission over detection at the review gate or inside the pass.** Admission is single-writer, holds the lineage, and precedes ladder cost; in-pass detection trusts untrusted execution; gate-time detection spends a review to learn the work was doomed. Cost accepted: the admission path gains a lineage lookup, bounded by the store's content-address index.
- **Exact content identity over similarity.** Mechanical over judgment. Cost accepted: trivially varied resubmission evades the detector; the @4 bounds still exhaust behind it, and the exact-repeat class is the designed and observed one.
- **Both-directions guards over firing-only guards.** Vacuity refusal costs derived-control fixtures and larger guards. Accepted: the @2 incident is the standing proof that a guard which cannot fail certifies nothing.
- **Arm-isolating mootness controls over one compound fixture.** The era-bounded direction gets a control built to differ from the on-path control in exactly the era bound, and the predicate's satisfied-request counterexample gets its own control rather than standing in for that direction. Cost accepted: two more single-delta controls in the tree and the manifest. Bought: each arm of the consumed State.Blocks evaluation is witnessed in isolation — a compound fixture, moot on two arms at once, is satisfiable by a classifier blind to either arm, which is REV-008's defect in fixture form.
- **Explicit clause-five check execution over transitive trust in the registry.** Cost accepted: one more named check in acceptance_checks, a pinned registry version, and an exemplar control in the fixture tree. Bought: D0005.C9's complete-check closure holds for this packet — the changed selector is exercised in both directions, on the tree it ships with, in the verification that admits it; an unchanged or broken selector cannot ride in behind four passing guards (REV-006).
- **Full copied stores over distilled fixtures.** The stores preserve the incident's replay realism; distillation would risk encoding the mechanism's assumptions into its own test bed. Cost accepted: repository weight of four stores, bounded and one-time.
- **Admitted-evidence pins plus manifest over dual-role fixtures.** Lawfulness under D0001.C2 costs an observation-pass record content obligation and one checked-in manifest file; it buys distinct invalidation semantics per role and a never-edit rule that fails mechanically instead of morally.
- **Same-packet documentation over follow-on documentation.** Cost accepted: this packet's change sets carry prose surfaces through the same gates as its mechanisms, and the packet is larger for it. Bought: D0001.C13 holds at close — the alternative is the forbidden state itself, implementation silently outrunning explanation, discoverable only by the next reader the docs were meant to serve (REV-007).
- **Three obligated Diátaxis modes over all four.** A tutorial for machinery no one lawfully hand-drives would be obligation theater. Cost accepted: the exclusion must be defended — and it is, in the artifact, with the audience argument stated, so the obligation set is checkable rather than aspirational.
- **Consuming existing determinations over local reimplementation.** The classifier couples to the fold's evaluation surface, the detector to the review machinery's, the closure constraint to the existing admission-completeness evaluation; that coupling is the correctness — divergence between two blockedness, two applicability, or two completeness notions is precisely the bug class P2 forbids.

## Rejected alternatives {#el:rejected-alternatives}

- **A served-only totality postcondition** — the refused predecessor's C1, evaluating only requests the session serves. Rejected: it admits a silent plan close over an unserved live request in the five-fact dead-end, which directly falsifies clause eight's plan-grain totality — the predicate binds on what a session *leaves* (REV-004's named failure). The full live-request range replaces it.
- **A mechanically guarded serve-everything invariant** (REV-004's second disposition arm: guarantee every live request visible to a plan close is served). Rejected in favor of the first arm: it couples close truthfulness to scheduling policy — every session must serve every live request or fabricate a service fact — changing selection behavior where clause eight demands only truthful close accounting; it would itself need a guard over a scheduling seam this packet otherwise leaves unchanged; and it is strictly more machinery for the same postcondition, which the full-range evaluation computes directly from close-state facts.
- **A new escalation reason code or a new "stall record" kind.** Rejected: closed vocabularies and closed kind set (D0001.C6/C10, D0003.C2/C7); the predicate mandates `bounds_exhausted` with budget kind and scope for clause eight; N11 forbids new ontology where existing concepts hold the role.
- **A dedicated oscillation reason code resolved from RFC 0006's fuller text.** Rejected: that text is not in the producing contract's declared environment, so resolving a code from it is an unpinned read (hermeticity), and deferring the resolution is the conditional core behavior D0003.C3 forbids and review 71896 refused. Minting is forbidden outright. The pinned vocabulary names a budget-family member whose semantics the firing genuinely has — provable doom escalating earlier, the @4 pattern — and C5 binds to it unconditionally.
- **Oscillation firings in the attempt basis** — the refused predecessor's placement: the producing runs cited as the attempt set, the shared content address with production count presented as the aggregated signature. Rejected: REV-005's named failure. The producing runs expressly succeeded, a content address is not a terminal failure signature, and an adverse verdict is not derived from an attempt terminal record — the placement cannot satisfy the attempt basis's own derivation invariant without the fabricated provenance REV-001 retired, and a faithful @4 guard must reject the record it produces. Reversed: the oscillation basis carries what clause ten actually obliges, and the attempt basis is restricted to actual attempt-terminal failure signatures.
- **Fabricating attempt-derived provenance for zero-attempt or successful-attempt records** — a synthetic aggregation "derived from an attempt set of size zero," synthetic attempt records, or success relabeled as failure. Rejected: REV-001's and REV-005's named failures; derivation claims must be true of the cited records. The three-basis partition keeps every guard honest instead.
- **Weakening or amending the @4 clause-five bound to admit empty summaries or non-failure signatures.** Rejected and not needed: the target holds clauses one through seven unamended; the bound's own subject term already scopes it to escalations written on budget exhaustion, and the partition preserves its full force over exactly that class.
- **Trusting the advanced clause-five check transitively** — advancing its subject selection in the registry but leaving it outside this packet's acceptance_checks, as the refused predecessor did. Rejected: REV-006's named failure — an unchanged or broken selector can ship while all four named checks pass, violating D0005.C9's exact complete-check closure and defeating the target's acceptance-check binding; "verified over the same materialized tree" must be a named-check fact, not an assertion. The check is named, registry-pinned, and executed against all three basis classes in this packet (C10).
- **Dual-role fixtures** — checked-in stores as simultaneously Evidence and Product Artifact. Rejected: D0001.C2's disjoint roles with distinct invalidation semantics; REV-002. Replaced by C9's single role plus C15's admitted-evidence pins.
- **Demoting the preservation claim to non-normative wording.** Rejected: preservation is a load-bearing motive of this packet — the counterexamples are perishable — and the admitted-evidence representation keeps the claim normative and lawful; softening it would abandon the motive to save a sentence.
- **Conditional conflict-routing inside mechanisms** (the once-refused form: "route to amendment if the vocabulary lacks the member"). Rejected: design-stage decision ambiguity deferred to delivery (D0003.C3); every dependency is resolved within pinned texts, and standing amendment law needs no restatement as a branch of any mechanism.
- **An out-of-band stall watcher or monitor process.** Rejected: a new component absorbing noticing authority (pinned D0002.C3 ownership), racing the session it watches, and inevitably timing-heuristic — judgment by another name.
- **Heuristic stall inference** (plan-count thresholds, wall-clock timeouts, "looks stuck" scoring). Rejected: Driver judgment, forbidden by the standing doctrine; the subtraction (a)–(e) is the specification that makes judgment unnecessary.
- **Extending the starvation warning to zero-run plans.** Rejected: a second overlapping detector at the wrong grain emitting a warning where a typed, diagnosable escalation is required; clause eight covers the blind spot with the stronger record.
- **Auto-recovery from the stall configuration** (auto-cancelling, auto-rebuilding, auto-resolving the blocking state). Rejected: pre-scripted resolution, forbidden outright (Principal rule, 2026-07-06); this packet adds raisers and classifiers, never resolvers.
- **Incremental annotation of recovery branches** (diagnosis where convenient, boolean elsewhere). Rejected: leaves silent claims expressible; the convention survives wherever it is not yet converted.
- **Free-prose diagnosis.** Rejected: unguardable beyond non-emptiness, unconsumable by aggregation and dedupe, unreadable as a stable citation; structure carries the content, prose may accompany it.
- **Context-blind oscillation detection** (content hash alone). Rejected: misfires on the lawful-regeneration class this identity's own lineage exercises, manufacturing judgment-requiring escalations; ADV-001's finding, adopted at proposal and binding here.
- **A detector-local applicability notion** (own freshness rules, own context comparison). Rejected: a parallel force rule is a P2 shadow; the detector consumes the one recomputation that exists, through one declared interface.
- **A new quiescence label for wedged states** ("wedged", "stalled"). Rejected: no vocabulary growth; with clause eight in force, `awaiting_principal` backed by an on-path stall escalation says it better — the label cites its evidence.
- **The satisfied-request form as the canonical era-bounded fixture** — witnessing the era-bounded mootness direction only through the predicate's satisfied-request counterexample. Rejected: that configuration's mootness is overdetermined — a satisfied request's escalation already recomputes moot on the path-and-liveness arm, era bound or none — so a guard built on it alone stays satisfiable by an era-blind classifier, the exact escape REV-008 names. The isolating era-bound control is the canonical era-bounded fixture; the satisfied-request configuration is witnessed by its own control as the predicate's named counterexample, not as the arm's proof (C9, C10).
- **Keeping fixtures env-gated in /tmp with a fetch or setup step.** Rejected: guards must run from the repository alone; evidence for the retired class must not depend on the retired forensics practice; durability is the promotion's entire point.
- **Distilled minimal fixtures instead of the preserved stores.** Rejected as primary material (kept only as derived controls beside the primaries): distillation forfeits replay realism and lets the mechanism's assumptions leak into its own test bed.
- **Deferring documentation to a follow-on packet, or leaving it to surrounding machinery.** Rejected: REV-007's named failure — it permits the capability to be integrated while explanation silently lags implementation, the state D0001.C13 expressly forbids; and per review 71935's minority note, obligations absent from normative design text do not exist for later stages. The explanation travels in this packet's own lineage or the close does not compute.
- **A documentation-specific gate.** Rejected: D0001.C13 admits explanation through the existing gate classes; a new gate is ontology growth with no role unheld (N11), and gate proliferation would dilute the gate vocabulary this repository keeps closed.
- **Commentary outside the graph as the explanation** (READMEs beside code, wiki pages, commit messages). Rejected: D0001.C13's own exclusion — explanation is canonical Diátaxis documentation, itself a Product Artifact in the graph; commentary outside the graph has no admission, no staleness, and no gate.
- **All four Diátaxis modes obligated regardless of subject matter.** Rejected: a tutorial for machinery that lawful operation never hand-drives is obligation theater — a surface with no learner. The obligated set is decided and defended at design, where the exclusion is reviewable, instead of discovered empty at delivery.
- **Prompt-wording and per-instance patching as the standing answer** (the 336285a class). Rejected as mechanism: the five 07-21 patches stand as shipped, and this design adds the class-level mechanics that make their successors unnecessary; instance-patching is the anti-pattern the predicate names.

## Decision (clauses) {#el:decision-clauses}

- **C1 — Plan-grain totality postcondition, over every live request.** Every planning session evaluates, for every live request in the store at its close — served by this session or not — the stall configuration on the state its close would produce: (a) the request's unmet executable conjunct set is non-empty; (b) the close leaves zero prepared runs serving the request; (c) no open run serves the request; (d) no scheduling deferral scoped to the request carries a retry horizon in the future; (e) no unresolved escalation's blocking scope lies on the request's dependency path. For each live request in stall configuration the session appends a typed escalation, and every such escalation precedes the plan record's append; the plan record is still written. No service, selection, or scheduling scope narrows the postcondition's range. Lawful waits and progress are subtracted by construction, and silence outside the stall configuration remains lawful.
- **C2 — Stall escalation form.** The C1 escalation carries reason code `bounds_exhausted`, scope the stalled request (which is its blocking scope), and the member of the closed `budget_kind` vocabulary that names attempt budgets — the member the @4 attempt-budget escalations already carry, read at its degenerate case: a frontier admitting zero further attempts is an attempt budget exhausted at zero. Its evidence is the frontier basis (C14): a diagnosis carried verbatim over an empty cited-attempt set — the frontier-consumption diagnosis where this session's recovery seam consumed the request's frontier, the service fall-through diagnosis (C3) where the close leaves the request unserved. Nothing executed, so nothing is cited: the record carries no aggregated failure-signature summary, claims no attempt-terminal derivation, and neither satisfies nor is subject to the @4 clause-five obligation, whose accepted subjects are escalations written on budget exhaustion (C14). It enters the existing self-dedupe unchanged. No reason code, budget kind, record type, or suppression logic is added.
- **C3 — Typed frontier consumption, total over live requests.** Every branch in the planner's recovery seam family returns the frontier claim type: pass, or consumed carrying a diagnosis that names the claiming branch and the failed proof by stable identifiers with the request (and, where narrower, the conjunct or element) as subject. A consumed claim without a diagnosis is unrepresentable — the type admits no bare-boolean constructor — and the seam's accounting is total over live requests, service included: every path that ends selection for a served request with nothing prepared yields a diagnosis, explicit or terminal-fall-through, and every live request the close leaves unserved yields the service fall-through diagnosis, whose branch identifier names the service-selection seam and whose proof identifier names the selection rule that left the request unserved. The return-true-emit-nothing convention is retired across the family, "not served" without a diagnosis included.
- **C4 — Diagnosis shape binds at construction.** The escalation writer refuses an empty or ill-formed diagnosis. Branch and proof identifiers — the service-selection seam's included — are stable constants of the seam; renaming one is a semantic change to the seam, and the structured fields are never replaced by prose.
- **C5 — Oscillation detection at the admission seam.** Where a produced candidate of an identity and pass would open its review ladder, the Driver evaluates: does a prior candidate of the same identity and pass carry the same content address — the artifact store's address, no other hash notion — and an adverse verdict (`needs_revision` or `reject`, from that pass's review or acceptance gate) whose force recomputes binding under the consumed force interface (el:definitions)? If so, that is livelock regardless of remaining bounds, and the session appends one escalation: reason code `bounds_exhausted`; scope the request the producing run served (its blocking scope); `budget_kind` the attempt-budget member — the ladder's bounded revision attempts are the budget the proof forecloses, escalated early exactly as the accepted @4 short-circuit escalates provable repetition without burning the remainder; evidence the oscillation basis (C14), carrying the new candidate's version, every content-identical predecessor version, and the binding adverse verdict record(s), with the new candidate's producing run cited as scope provenance only — no failure signature claimed, no aggregated summary present, no fact of the successful producing runs relabeled as failure. The review ladder does not open; the produced candidate version remains recorded; remaining bounds are neither consulted for firing nor extended. Content equality and verdict force are individually necessary and jointly sufficient; success of the producing runs is no defense. The normative source for this clause's semantics is the pinned @5 predicate's clause-ten text; no other RFC text is consumed, and no behavior is conditional on one.
- **C6 — The non-firing direction binds equally.** A content-address match whose predecessor adverse verdicts all recompute forceless admits exactly as today — no escalation, no record of the non-firing; lawful regeneration after a context move proceeds. The detector holds no applicability logic of its own and never gives a forceless verdict operative force.
- **C7 — Quiescence consults blocking scope.** A quiescent session reports `awaiting_principal` iff an unresolved escalation's blocking scope lies on a live request's dependency path, evaluated by consuming the fold's existing State.Blocks mootness evaluation. Off-path, era-bounded, and satisfied-request-scoped escalations never pin the label; unresolved escalations remain visible through the existing derived status surface regardless of classification; no new label is minted; the starvation warning stands unamended, its zero-run blind spot covered by C1.
- **C8 — One rule, one source, its consumers.** Blocking-scope mootness is evaluated only by the fold's rule and consumed by the fold, by C1's conjunct (e), and by C7. Verdict force is evaluated only by the review machinery's D0005.C10 recomputation — the rule binding here as the accepted proposal restates it — and consumed through the single interface `force(v, K) → {binding, forceless}` by review and by C5/C6. Content identity is only the artifact store's content address. The oscillation semantics are single-sourced from the pinned @5 predicate's clause-ten text. Admission completeness is evaluated only by the existing `capability-admission-complete` machinery (C16). No consumer re-derives, divergently caches, or shadows these facts.
- **C9 — Fixture doctrine.** The four preserved RQ-57950 copied stores enter the repository as Product Artifacts in exactly one role — primary fixtures, the standing guards' replay material — bytes as captured, credential-free asserted at admission (scrub under a documented delta or refuse; never silent), one directory per store under the standing-guards tree, readable repository-relative with no environment gate, /tmp path, or network. Derived controls — minimal documented single-delta mutations of a named primary, checked in beside it and listed in the fixture manifest — supply configurations the primaries do not exhibit, at minimum: a lawful-wait control; a context-move control; an on-path control; an era-bound control (the on-path control's added escalation carrying additionally an era bound on which the fold's State.Blocks evaluation excludes it — otherwise on-path, its blocking scope on a live request's dependency path, moot solely by the era-bound arm; the on-path/era-bound pair differs in exactly that bound and so isolates the arm); a satisfied-request control (an unresolved era-bounded inert escalation whose blocking scope lies on a satisfied request's dependency path, in a store still holding live requests — the predicate's clause-eleven counterexample); an unserved-stall control (a live request in stall configuration left outside the session's served set); and an attempt-basis exemplar control (a lawful @4-class attempt-budget escalation configuration with cited attempt terminal failure records). Primaries are never edited to make a guard pass; a missing configuration is a derived control, never a primary edit; an edit fails mechanically against the manifest and C15's pins.
- **C10 — Standing guards, and the advanced clause-five check bound with them.** Four guards, one per @5 clause, with entrypoints `go run ./tools/standing-guards/<guard>`, registered in the check registry's `stalls-guards` set (registry version advancing per its own discipline). The delivery packet's acceptance_checks name all four under acceptance-checks-bind@1, together with the @4 clause-five registered check at its advanced registry identity — the registry version whose subject selection consumes the C14 discriminator — and the packet pins that registry version, so the name resolves only to the advanced check and an unadvanced registry fails work-graph legality. Verification executes every named check over the same materialized tree. Each guard replays the wired seam over fixtures rather than unit-testing an extracted function, verifies its consumed fixtures against the checked-in manifest before replay, and contains at least one firing and one non-firing assertion — a guard that cannot observe its mechanism's absence is refused at design. Guard eight asserts the C2 escalation form (frontier basis included), its append preceding the plan record, the plan record's existence, firing over the unserved-stall control with the service fall-through diagnosis, and silence over the lawful-wait control. Guard nine asserts diagnosis shape — frontier-consumption and service fall-through cases both — and the writer's construction-time refusals across all three bases (C14). Guard ten asserts C5's firing — the escalation's oscillation-basis form: both versions, the binding verdict, scope provenance, no summary or failure-signature field; no ladder opens — and C6's non-firing over the context-move control. Guard eleven asserts `awaiting_principal` withheld over the primaries as captured; withheld over the era-bound control, whose escalation is otherwise on a live request's dependency path and excluded solely by the fold's era-bound evaluation — the assertion that isolates the era-bound arm, the era-bound control differing from the on-path control in exactly that bound; withheld over the satisfied-request control, the predicate's satisfied-request counterexample; and reported over the on-path control or a primary's post-guard-eight state. The advanced clause-five check's run demonstrates its selection against all three basis classes: full @4 obligations evaluated over attempt-basis records (the attempt-basis exemplar control at minimum), and no frontier-basis or oscillation-basis record selected — the changed selector is exercised in both directions on the tree it ships with.
- **C11 — Authority untouched.** No mechanism in this design resolves an escalation, crosses or weakens a gate or claim, overrides a bound, or pre-scripts a resolution; bounds are touched only in the escalate-earlier direction; every escalation these mechanisms append is read and adjudicated individually by whoever actually holds the authority (Principal rule, 2026-07-06).
- **C12 — Determinism and derived state.** Each mechanism is a total, deterministic function of the store at its seam — no timing, thresholds, similarity, or discretion; replay recomputes identical classifications and identical firing decisions; everything appended is an ordinary ledger record under existing record types (D0003.C7); every runtime consumer of these facts remains derived and rebuildable (pinned D0002.C2). Every decision citation in this design resolves within the pinned environment on its face (el:environment-pins).
- **C13 — Unconditional design; amendment path untouched.** Every mechanism in this design is unconditional at its seam: no designed behavior branches on the content of any text outside the pinned environment and pinned inputs, and no decision of this design is deferred to a later stage (D0003.C3). This design amends nothing — the @4 bound, the @5 predicate, RFC 0006's text, and D0005.C10 stand as accepted, changing only on the lawful amendment path — and per the accepted proposal's constraint, no mechanism here gives a forceless verdict operative force, and none is designed to. That accepted texts change only by amendment is standing law restated, not a conditional of any mechanism.
- **C14 — Three evidence bases and jointly-total guard subjects.** Every escalation carrying reason code `bounds_exhausted` carries exactly one evidence basis, fixed by its writing trigger and discriminable from the record alone. The attempt basis — @4 clause-five exhaustions and clause-six short-circuits — carries a non-empty cited set of attempt terminal records and the aggregated failure-signature summary derived from exactly those records, and is restricted to actual attempt-terminal failures: no successful run's record grounds it, and no production fact, content address, or verdict citation is presented as a failure signature. The frontier basis — the C1/C2 stall class — carries an empty cited-attempt set and the frontier-consumption or service fall-through diagnosis. The oscillation basis — C5 firings — carries the new candidate's version, every content-identical predecessor version, and the binding adverse verdict record(s), with the producing run cited as scope provenance only. The writer refuses a record with no basis or more than one; an attempt basis with an empty citation set, an empty summary, or a cited non-failure record; a frontier basis with a non-empty citation set or an absent or ill-formed diagnosis; an oscillation basis with an absent new version, an empty predecessor set, or an absent or non-adverse verdict citation. Guard subjects partition accordingly: the @4 clause-five guard binds exactly attempt-basis records — its accepted subject term, "an escalation written on budget exhaustion … derived from its attempts' terminal records" — with obligations unchanged and un-weakened; guard nine binds exactly frontier-basis records; guard ten binds the oscillation-basis form of C5 firings; the constructor refusals make the three obligations jointly total over `bounds_exhausted` records, so no record evades all and none answers to another class's obligation. The registered clause-five check's subject selection consumes the discriminator under the check registry's own versioning discipline with assertions unchanged, and its advanced identity is named, pinned, and executed in this packet per C10; no delivered state pairs any basis's writer with a selection that has not consumed the discriminator — one designed invariant of one record shape, verified over one materialized tree. The partition is located in the accepted predicate's own text (clause eight's code mandate, clause nine's separate diagnosis guard, clause ten's "carrying both versions", the @4 bound's trigger-scoped subject term); no clause is amended.
- **C15 — Incident evidence, pins, and manifest.** The incident-preservation claim is carried by admitted evidence, never by the fixtures: the world-authored capture of the four RQ-57950 stores and their entry into this repository are recorded by exogenous change record(s) — the admitted evidence kind the target state's own `evidence_admitted` conjunct names (D0003.C2) — carrying explicit content pins for every primary (the checked-in tree's content address), capture provenance (request, window, source store), and the documented delta for any scrub; record grain follows the observation pass's standing convention, with this clause fixing content obligations only. A checked-in fixture manifest in the standing-guards tree lists every primary's pinned content address and every derived control's primary and delta; guards verify consumed fixtures against the manifest (repository-only), and manifest-to-evidence-record correspondence is verified on the graph side, never by guards. Roles never merge: stores and manifest are Product Artifacts with product invalidation semantics; the exogenous change records are Evidence with evidence semantics (D0001.C2); an edit to a primary changes its address and fails loudly against both pin sets.
- **C16 — Canonical documentation is part of the capability.** The capability this design delivers carries canonical Diátaxis documentation as Product Artifacts in the repository's canonical documentation surface, within this packet's product lineage: explanation (the stall doctrine — the computed wedged/waiting subtraction and its lawful silences, mechanical triggers upholding the no-judgment doctrine, oscillation as content identity composed with verdict force, the three-basis evidence honesty, the quiescence label as citation), reference (the stall configuration facts and their full live-request range, the frontier claim type with its constructors and stable identifier sets including the service fall-through case, the three evidence bases with required content and constructor refusals, the oscillation trigger and record form with the consumed force interface signature, the quiescence rule, the fixture manifest format and tree layout, the guard entrypoints and the `stalls-guards` set with the advanced clause-five check), and how-to (adjudicating a stall escalation from the escalation and its causal records alone). The tutorial mode is deliberately unobligated, with the audience reasons stated in this design (el:documentation-surface). These artifacts are carried by this packet's change sets, verified over the same materialized tree, integrated with the same head, and admitted through the existing gate classes; no documentation-specific gate and no new artifact kind is minted; documentation is never commentary outside the graph, uses the pinned vocabulary, and names no backend or model (P9). Under D0001.C13 the @5 close computes `[satisfied: Verified]` only over a tree whose explanation is admitted alongside implementation and justification — the conjunction evaluated by the existing admission-completeness machinery, with no completeness notion added here (P2).

## Consequences {#el:consequences}

- Silent zero-run stalls become unwritable on the lawful path for every live request the close leaves: a wedged request — served or unserved — gets its escalation appended before the plan record, carrying branch and proof. The 2026-07-21 class retires as a class, the unserved variant with it; the five patches stand, and their successors are unnecessary.
- Wedged-versus-waiting becomes one ledger read: waiting states name their wait through conjuncts (b)–(e); wedged states carry an on-path stall escalation. Copied-store forensics for this class is retired.
- Every `bounds_exhausted` record is self-classifying among three honest bases: attempt-basis records answer to the @4 guard in full force over actual terminal failures; frontier-basis records answer to guard nine; oscillation-basis records answer to guard ten with the versions and verdict clause ten obliges. Hollow or mislabeled evidence does not construct in any basis, no record evades its guard, and no successful fact is ever dressed as failure. The @4 clause-five bound is neither weakened nor evaded, and the @2-class hollow record cannot recur in this family.
- The advanced clause-five check ships only exercised: named in this packet's acceptance_checks at a pinned registry identity and run in both directions — obligations over the attempt-basis exemplar, exclusion over the other two classes — on the same materialized tree as the guards. D0005.C9's complete-check closure holds for this packet.
- `awaiting_principal` becomes a citation rather than a default: every report of it is backed by an on-path unresolved escalation, and clause eight guarantees the wedged case produces one. Off-path, era-bounded, and satisfied-request-scoped escalations stop pinning the graph's label while remaining visible in the status surface — each mootness direction witnessed by guard eleven, the era-bound arm in isolation.
- Revision loops end at the first lawful repeat, under the existing budget vocabulary — no vocabulary grows: review and acceptance attention is never spent on content already refused in a standing context, while lawful regeneration after context moves proceeds untouched. The prompt-wording-patch class of loop-ending is retired.
- The recovery seam family bears a total type over live requests: no future branch can claim a frontier silently, no close can leave a request unserved silently, and branch and proof identifiers become part of the ledger's standing vocabulary of explanation.
- The `stalls-guards` set grows by four registered checks and advances one; under acceptance-checks-bind@1, satisfaction of @5 cannot recur without the guards and the advanced check executing and passing over checked-in fixtures — the close computes `[satisfied: Verified]` through the anchored funnel, never asserts it.
- The four RQ-57950 stores become permanent regression material, with the incident's evidentiary claim carried by admitted evidence whose content pins make any primary edit mechanically loud; the incident's diagnosability no longer depends on any retired practice or any role-ambiguous artifact.
- The capability arrives explained: canonical Diátaxis explanation, reference, and how-to surfaces enter the same product lineage through the same gates, so the D0001.C13 conjunction holds at close and implementation cannot silently outrun explanation. The adjudicator the target state names has the how-to that makes the escalation records operable.
- Adjudication reads escalations, not stores: each stall arrives naming its branch, its proof, or its oscillating versions and binding verdict — the resolution note is writable from the escalation and its causal records alone, which is this target state's core promise and the material a later existence-proof predicate version will recount.
- Every consumer of this design resolves its citations within the environment pinned on its face; no gate or successor stage inherits an unpinned dependency from it.
- Costs accepted and bounded: a per-close postcondition evaluation over every live request, on facts already in hand; an admission-time content-address lineage lookup; the repository weight of four stores, seven controls, a manifest, and the documentation surfaces; a one-time total type change across the recovery seam family; one advanced registered check named and pinned in this packet's acceptance_checks.
