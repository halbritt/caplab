## Executive verdict

The campaign ended correctly. RQ-57950 satisfied the exact mechanically
complete `semantic-and-durable-closure@2` predicate. The ledger remained
append-only and coherent, the final Product had two independent admitted
verification signals, the exact Change Set was applied to the current Product
head, and the closing plan had no unmet conjuncts. No evidence indicates lost
ledger records, corrupted artifact bodies, a bypassed gate, or manual admission
of the payload.

The run was nevertheless operationally unacceptable. The exact campaign name
was active for 11 days 14 hours 18 minutes; RQ-57950 alone was active for 8
days 17 hours 45 minutes. That elapsed time was not explained by necessary
model execution or test time. It included repeated Driver defects, incomplete
cross-generation contracts, 20 direct request escalations, crash loops, silent
zero-run plans, ineffective wakes, mixed runtime deployments, unhealthy lanes
left running for hours, and long intervals in which driving agents reported
status or waited for authority that was either already present or irrelevant to
the active dependency cone.

Five failure families dominated:

1. **A stop had no effect contract.** “Running,” “armed,” “waiting,” and
   “awaiting principal” were accepted without naming the exact ledger or spool
   effect that should occur, or a deadline after which its absence was a
   defect. Human attention became the liveness detector.
2. **Exact context was implemented seam by seam, not end to end.** Target,
   decision, packet, review, retirement, application, and revalidation
   identities were each correct locally while their composition failed on
   historical or moving graph states.
3. **Recovery completeness was not a standing invariant.** The Driver could
   observe an unmet executable predicate and still emit zero runs, no useful
   deferral, and no scoped escalation. Four distinct forms appeared on the
   final day after earlier forms had already been repaired.
4. **Deployment generation was procedural, not mechanically indivisible.** A
   correct Driver paired with a stale local backend generated invalid work;
   completed repairs also sat unintegrated or undeployed while the broken
   runtime continued to be observed.
5. **Driving-agent control was conversational.** Monitoring promises, handoff
   state, review clocks, continuation duties, and authority boundaries lived in
   chat rather than in durable effect-and-deadline rules. Different models and
   effort settings reproduced the same control failure.

The registers below contain all 20 direct RQ-57950 escalations, 45 additional
technical incident entries, and 21 driving-agent or operational failures found
in the retained evidence. Those categories overlap where one event crossed
more than one boundary; they are not an additive event count.

This was a bootstrap campaign: the compiler was using its own incomplete
durability and semantic-closure machinery to prove that machinery complete.
The deepening run was expected to expose defects. That explains the sequence
of new edge cases; it does not excuse hours of undetected idleness, failure to
replace overdue lanes, status-as-termination, or split-generation deployment.

The campaign was enabling evidence for a later
`progress-needs-no-principal@1` window; it did not satisfy that separate target.
The present repository ruling still says the earlier July 4–6 window failed
that predicate. RQ-57950 closed semantic and durability machinery that a future
unattended progress window can exercise.

## Deep causal analysis

### Causal hierarchy

The proximate causes were specific defects: nil/empty fold representations,
wrong anchor prefixes, missing retirement fields, stale implementation
selection, misplaced quality requirements, or incomplete planner branches.
Treating those as independent mistakes would miss why the run kept finding the
next one.

The primary systemic cause was **partial closure of cross-cutting invariants**.
The accepted architecture required exact context and resumable durable effects,
but implementation and tests typically closed one producer/consumer pair at a
time. The live request composed:

```text
accepted target
  -> exact Plan authority
  -> moving Work Graph and claimed packet continuity
  -> sealed pass implementation and backend declaration
  -> review and verification force
  -> root Change Set and current Product application
  -> fold snapshot and interrupted-session replay
```

Every arrow carried identity, freshness, implementation, and recovery
semantics. A seam could pass its own tests while still disagreeing with the
writer before it, the historical record behind it, or the moving head after
it. The writer/checker fixture defect and the mixed Driver/backend deployment
are the cleanest examples.

The primary operational cause was **absence of a wait-effect contract**. The
system had clocks, timer floors, lane budgets, statuses, and supervisors, but
the driving practice did not consistently say:

```text
I am waiting for record or spool transition X.
It must occur by time or horizon Y.
If it does not, recovery action Z is already authorized.
```

As a result, the mechanism that should have detected liveness failure was the
Principal asking for status. This converted recoverable defects into multi-hour
idle windows.

The strongest contributing causes were:

- **Recovery was enumerated, not total.** Each repaired zero-plan branch left
  other ways for an unmet predicate to own the frontier without emitting a run
  or blocker.
- **Semantic generation lacked a single attested envelope.** Checkout head,
  catalogs, registry, policy, binary, and runtime siblings could disagree even
  though each individual hash or binary was internally valid.
- **Tests favored constructed local shapes.** Production writers, historical
  ledgers, interrupted cuts, cold folds, snapshot folds, and moving heads were
  underrepresented until the live graph supplied them.
- **Authority reasoning was not dependency-local.** A real Principal-only
  transition elsewhere was interpreted as permission to stop the entire drive.
- **The control loop ended at commentary boundaries.** Status updates were
  treated as terminal responses rather than checkpoints inside an authorized
  continuing task.
- **Non-authoritative observations were trusted too far.** Plane and dossier
  projections, plus systemd labels, PIDs, CPU activity, and chat claims, were
  sometimes treated as lifecycle state rather than evidence to reconcile
  against the artifact graph, ledger, and sealed manifests.

### What was not the root cause

- **The ledger was not corrupt.** It refused invalid work, preserved every
  durable record, and supported cold replay and final proof.
- **The Principal was not the general blocker.** A narrow D0010 amendment and
  a few personal acceptances were genuinely required. Most lost time came from
  applying those boundaries globally or failing to continue unrelated
  mechanical work.
- **Vendor/model quality was not the general blocker.** Quota, context, and a
  weak review lane caused specific incidents. Stronger models and the complete
  backend pool improved useful work, but several major stalls occurred before
  model invocation or inside deterministic Driver code.
- **One bad driving agent was not the general blocker.** Different agents,
  harnesses, models, and effort settings repeated status-as-stop and
  effect-free waiting. The missing control contract survived agent replacement.
- **The D0010 continuity refusal was not erroneous.** The old law correctly
  refused movement it had no authority to allow. The defect was the absence of
  a narrow accepted-Plan authority channel; the Principal-approved amendment
  was the correct constitutional change.
- **More retries would not have helped.** Many failures were deterministic.
  Attempt 24 after 21 `claimed_packet_moved` refusals, another stale
  packetization implementation, or another sub-floor review would have spent
  time without changing the preimage.

## Recovery, idempotency, concurrency, and partial-write traces

### 1. Liveness and observation (FMA-003)

**Invariant.** Every drive session re-arms the durable wake floor, and every
wait is judged by the compiler or lane effect it should produce, not by the
existence of the mechanism intended to produce it.

**Observed order.** Reload-sensitive timers, observer lock contention, session
crashes before `drive_session_record`, a ledger-only watcher, and agents that
did not maintain their promised watch all converged on silent time. The most
severe examples were 65 failed wake services over roughly 19 hours, 27
stagnant timer sessions over 7h37, the 6h40 snapshot crash loop, and the 5h54
ledger freeze while the timer looked active.

**Why detection failed.** Timer state, future trigger, process existence, and
ledger silence were each interpreted separately. No one observation asked
whether the expected next record arrived inside the declared horizon. The
terminal-event watcher was especially vulnerable: a crash before append made
silence indistinguishable from healthy waiting.

**Recovery that worked.** Cross-checking ledger position, unit failures, spool
manifest, lane clock, output movement, and installed revision localized the
problem. `OnCalendar`, lock narrowing, typed session failure, and fold-snapshot
parity removed observed mechanisms.

**Smallest recurrence control.** For every active wait, persist or display an
effect tuple `(expected_effect, deadline, recovery_owner)`. At two wake floors
without either semantic movement or a typed quiescent/blocking record, a
one-shot health check should fail visibly. It must remain observation: it may
alert or open a defect dossier, never resolve authority or invent work.

### 2. Record-then-dispatch, durable prefixes, and derived state (FMA-001)

**Invariant.** A scheduling decision, binding, dispatch, submission, admission,
and closure must resume idempotently after every durable cut. Derived fold
state may accelerate replay but may not change its semantic result.

**Observed order.** Early pre-binding and partial-admission repairs made common
prefixes recoverable. Fold snapshots then preserved an in-memory nil/empty
shape that cold decode normalized differently. Strict deep equality converted
one half-finished dispatch into a permanent crash loop. A separate stale
review-candidate race turned a normal plan/run-open movement into 65 fatal wake
failures.

**Blast radius.** Because every drive folded the same ledger and reached the
same bad comparison, the failure was fleet-like for this graph: no packet,
review, or integration work could progress regardless of backend capacity.

**Recovery that worked.** The Derived-State Doctrine made snapshot deletion a
lawful unwedge after proof on a copied graph. Code then compared durable
same-sequence identity rather than representation accident. Later repairs
converted moving run-open witnesses into replanning rather than session death.

**Smallest recurrence control.** Every new derived-state representation needs
a three-way equivalence guard over the same copied live store: uninterrupted
fold, cold fold, and snapshot-plus-tail fold. Every record-then-dispatch state
needs a table-driven cut-point corpus produced by the real writer.

### 3. Exact authority, applicability, and moving lineage (FMA-004)

**Invariant.** Force follows exact, current, lawful context. A diagnostic pin
is not replacement authority; a claimed packet does not move unless the exact
accepted Plan lineage authorizes its changed element; stale narrative cannot
outrank the accepted current artifact.

**Observed order.** The campaign first fixed coarse target and request context.
The live graph then exposed element anchors (`#el:`), diagnostic/replacement
roles, retirement metadata, accepted-preimage selection, stale Product and
Work Graph force, withdrawn/rejected head precedence, and revision baseline.
The 21 continuity refusals were the lawful point at which accepted text itself
was incomplete.

**Why tests missed it.** Small fixtures had one head, one Plan, one
implementation, and little historical residue. The live graph had claimed
packets, rejected and accepted Plan lineages, stale but admissible diagnostics,
old implementations, and partially progressed root authority at once.

**Recovery that worked.** The Principal amended only D0010.C3's exact lineage
case. Subsequent code made role, element anchor, preimage, and baseline
explicit. Refusals continued to fail closed on every non-authorized move.

**Smallest recurrence control.** Maintain a production-derived lineage corpus
covering accepted, rejected, withdrawn, superseded, retired, unchanged, and
current authority in combination. A planner-owned unmet conjunct must either
select one lawful next effect or name the exact accepted artifact or Principal
act it lacks.

### 4. Scheduler quality and deployment generation (FMA-005)

**Invariant.** Scheduling chooses a capable lane; it cannot change gate
semantics. Every runtime component that renders or validates the sealed
contract must execute one compatible generation.

**Observed order.** Provisional tuple ranking omitted most strong build lanes.
Primary review placement selected a sub-floor lane that could not satisfy the
Strong licensing floor. Later, the Driver understood retirement metadata
while the installed local backend did not, so valid new planning dispatched
through an invalid old producer.

**Why detection failed.** Capability and ranking were checked separately from
the authority the result could earn. Deployment verified individual binaries,
not the closure of all siblings used by a run.

**Recovery that worked.** Primary reviews now declare Strong requirements; the
full single-agent build pool is ranked according to the Principal's supplied
ordering; matching backend binaries were installed together; implementation
selection rejects obsolete pass implementations.

**Smallest recurrence control.** A deployment receipt should enumerate Driver,
all reachable backend binaries, catalog/registry/policy digests, and source
revision. Resume driving only after a read-only parity probe proves the bundle
is one compatible generation. This strengthens existing D0007/D0009 delivery;
it must not turn scheduler rank into semantic authority.

### 5. Planner completeness (FMA-002)

**Invariant.** A request-scoped executable unmet conjunct must own one lawful
next effect: a prepared run, a typed deferral with a future horizon, or a scoped
escalation naming the exact missing authority or consumed frontier.

**Observed order.** `computeSteps` could return a nonempty `unmet` list while
preparing no run. `plan` then wrote a plan with `planned_runs: []`; later
sessions saw the same plan content and continued without another record. This
first stranded RQ-51460, then recurred in three root-authority shapes on Jul
21. Two adjacent recovery defects selected duplicate or ineffective runs
instead of the lawful useful effect.

**Why detection failed.** A plan record looked like Driver activity, but no
standing rule evaluated whether its executable unmet set had an owner. The
absence of a run, deferral, and escalation was visible only through a joint
inspection of the plan body and the target evaluator.

**Recovery that worked.** Copied-live-store witnesses preserved each exact
authority shape; `d9acba8`, `0d33f10`, `68a2125`, `336285a`, and `efff775`
repaired the observed branches without weakening target or gate semantics.

**Smallest recurrence control.** Check the completeness trichotomy at the end
of planning for every executable unmet conjunct and exercise it over a
production-derived head-state corpus. If no lawful effect exists, the Driver
must identify the precise blocker rather than append or retain an inert plan.

### Operational control trace — driving-agent continuation and authority

**Invariant.** A driving agent continues every already-authorized mechanical
path until the requested predicate is satisfied, an exact dependency requires
Principal authority, or a real external blocker prevents safe progress. A
status update does not satisfy any of those terminal conditions.

**Observed order.** Agents promised watches but yielded, let a 30-minute-p95
lane run 7h14, waited 6h23 for an acceptance without continuing independent
work, ended on status for another 4h42, and left ready repairs undeployed.
Repeated Principal prompts restored motion.

**Why changing models did not solve it.** The prompt supplied goals and safety
boundaries but did not encode an effect deadline, a continuation predicate, or
a durable monitor. Cautious models interpreted any record involving an
authority boundary as a global stop; status-oriented agents treated a report
as task completion.

**Smallest recurrence control.** Every driving prompt or handoff should carry a
literal continuation contract:

```text
Terminal only when the named target is graph-satisfied, an exact blocking
record in its dependency cone requires Principal action, or a demonstrated
external blocker prevents safe work. Every wait names its expected effect and
deadline. At deadline, inspect, abort/replace, repair, or escalate. Reporting
status never ends the drive.
```

This rule grants no new authority. It only prevents an agent from discarding
authority and work already present.

## What failed loudly or safely, and what worked

The result was recoverable because several foundational choices held under
pressure:

- **The ledger remained authoritative and intact.** Invalid work was refused;
  failures did not require record deletion or manual truth edits.
- **Derived state was disposable.** Snapshot deletion could be tested on a copy
  and used to recover without changing ledger meaning.
- **Exact gates protected semantics.** The 21 continuity refusals were costly,
  but they prevented a claimed packet from moving without accepted authority.
- **Copied Graph Stores were effective incident laboratories.** Strict fold,
  applicability, continuity, authority, and final bridge defects were
  reproduced without mutating the live graph.
- **Isolated worktrees preserved active lane state.** Later repairs avoided the
  shared-checkout loss pattern.
- **Small red/green repairs exposed the next boundary.** The final day moved
  quickly once each zero-plan shape had a copied-live-store witness.
- **Packetization eventually produced useful parallelism.** The original
  21-packet graph became a 30-packet split graph and then a 32-packet closing
  graph; the monolithic record-contract packet became ten independently
  schedulable contract packets.
- **Independent review corrected the postmortem itself.** It caught the wrong
  operation-key cause, observer-lock mechanism, timer claim, and refusal count.
- **The Principal held the semantic boundary.** Predicate weakening, implicit
  continuity authority, pre-scripted escalation handling, and manual payload
  integration were rejected.
- **The final proof was graph-native.** Closure did not depend on the final
  driving agent's narrative. Satisfaction 71380 can be independently decoded.

### Alternatives rejected by this review

- **Do nothing.** The realized recurrence includes multi-hour silent stalls,
  user-data loss, mixed-generation durable candidates, and Principal attention
  as the alert system. That rules out treating recurrence as negligible and
  justifies bounded estimates or prototypes for the P0 controls. This audit
  did not estimate implementation cost and therefore does not claim a proved
  cost-benefit result or select an implementation.
- **Add a new authority-bearing watcher or daemon.** D0006 chose no daemon;
  liveness observation can be a one-shot check invoked by existing timers or
  human tooling. It must not become a second graph or recovery verb.
- **Make one checkout socially privileged forever.** A single drive root is a
  useful interim operational rule, but the accepted direction is pinned,
  reconstructible semantics that tolerate independent actors.
- **Loosen validators to keep work moving.** The refusals prevented silent
  corruption. Repair missing authority or context propagation instead.
- **Add new escalation reason codes for every incident.** Existing
  `bounds_exhausted` plus budget kind, scope, and typed failure signatures can
  represent these cases; new closed vocabulary requires accepted change.
- **Pre-script escalation resolutions.** Each escalation remains individually
  read and adjudicated. Mechanical diagnosis may improve; authority does not.
- **Let Plane, branch names, marker files, systemd labels, or chat become
  lifecycle state.** Plane and dossier entries remain projections; the others
  are observations. None is lifecycle state under P10.
- **Choose a stronger model as the sole remedy.** Strong build and review lanes
  matter, but deterministic pre-model failures and status-as-stop behavior need
  control and runtime fixes.

## Closing assessment

RQ-57950 proved the target it was created to prove, but it also demonstrated
why “progress without Principal” cannot be reduced to choosing a stronger
agent. The compiler's semantic and durability boundaries needed repair, and
the agents responsible for driving it needed a control loop that survives
conversation, model, lane, timer, and process boundaries.

Accepted decisions and case-specific Principal acts supplied the necessary
authority. The artifact graph carried their exact pins and computed the final
truth. Time was lost where effects were not bounded and watched, where runtime
generation was not indivisible, and where an agent stopped despite an
authorized next action. The first controls to investigate should therefore be
the smallest ones that close those exact gaps: effect-and-deadline waits,
universal planner completeness, atomic runtime generation, production-shape
replay, and an explicit continuation predicate. Broader architecture should
remain unchanged until those controls produce new evidence.

This postmortem records and recommends. It does not amend an RFC, resolve an
escalation, close a Plane item, or confer satisfaction. Ledger record 71380 is
the terminal graph-computed proof of the outcome; like every provenance record,
it is not a source of authority.
