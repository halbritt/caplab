    "docs/stalls-need-no-judgment/explanation.md": "# Stalls Need No Judgment {#el:stalls-need-no-judgment}\n\nA healthy Driver does not ask the Principal to discover why work stopped. It either applies a declared mechanical recovery or writes one terminal escalation whose record already says what consumed the frontier and which proof failed. The Principal still decides authority questions; the machine supplies the diagnosis.\n\nThis doctrine is the stall-side complement of progress that needs no Principal. Progress machinery keeps work moving without a human noticing and initiating it. Stall machinery makes the remaining human decision readable without a human debugging the scheduler, replaying a copied store, or searching an object store.\n\n## The Plan-Close Totality Rule {#el:plan-close-totality}\n\nPlan close evaluates every live request, not only requests that happened to produce a step. A request is a zero-run dead-end when all of these facts hold at the same plan boundary:\n\n- At least one executable target conjunct remains unmet.\n- No prepared run serves the request.\n- No open run serves the request.\n- No scheduling deferral serving the request has a future retry horizon.\n- No unresolved escalation has a blocking scope on the request's dependency path.\n\nIf the conjunction is true, the Driver appends a `bounds_exhausted` escalation with budget kind `plan_frontier` before it appends the plan record. If any escalation append fails, the plan record is not written. An existing record with the same stable dedupe key suppresses a duplicate append.\n\nThe subtractions are deliberately narrow. A run or deferral for another request is not a wait for this request. An expired deferral is not a future horizon. The existence of an unresolved escalation is not enough; the ledger fold's dependency-aware `State.Blocks` result must say that the escalation blocks this live request.\n\n## A Consumed Frontier Is a Typed Result {#el:typed-frontier}\n\nA recovery branch no longer reports only that it consumed work. Its consuming result contains:\n\n- the live request identity;\n- the branch that consumed the frontier;\n- a stable failed-proof code;\n- the proposition the branch tried to establish;\n- the terminal observation; and\n- the fact that would have been required to proceed.\n\nThe consuming result is a closed variant: code cannot construct it without the diagnosis. Pass-through is a different variant. Total accounting rejects two branches claiming the same frontier and rejects duplicate reports from one branch.\n\nThe plan-frontier escalation copies this basis into its own record. An adjudicator can therefore read the escalation without reproducing the planning session.\n\n## Three Evidence Bases, One Escalation Kind {#el:evidence-bases}\n\n`bounds_exhausted` uses a discriminator to keep three causal shapes separate:\n\n| Evidence basis | Lawful budget kinds | Meaning |\n| --- | --- | --- |\n| `failure_signature_summary` | `attempt_total`, `deterministic_repeat` | Terminal attempt signatures, details, and observed counts |\n| `consumed_frontier` | `plan_frontier` | The zero-run recovery branch and failed proof |\n| `oscillation` | `oscillation` | The new candidate and a binding content-identical refused or rejected predecessor |\n\nConstructors reject mismatched pairs. A plan-frontier record cannot masquerade as attempt exhaustion, and an oscillation cannot omit either version.\n\nFor deterministic repetition, exactly one signature observed at least twice is enough to stop. Ordinary attempt exhaustion may aggregate several distinct signatures and their counts.\n\n## Oscillation Is About Content, Not Run Failure {#el:oscillation}\n\nA successful producing run can still livelock. Before opening a review ladder, admission compares a new candidate with binding prior candidates for the same identity and producing pass. If a refused or rejected predecessor has the same content hash, the new version is an oscillation even when attempt budget remains.\n\nThe detector chooses the newest matching adverse predecessor and writes both immutable versions, the shared content hash, the predecessor disposition, and its ledger sequence. It then appends an oscillation escalation instead of opening review.\n\nNonbinding history, accepted candidates, different identities, different passes, and different content hashes do not trigger the detector.\n\n## Quiescence Follows Blocking Scope {#el:quiescence}\n\n`awaiting_principal` is not a synonym for \u201can escalation exists.\u201d The classifier asks the fold whether each unresolved escalation blocks each live request. Confirm the same identity, pass, and content hash, distinct versions, a binding predecessor, and a refused or rejected predecessor disposition.\n\n## Decide the Authority Question, Not the Mechanism {#el:decide-authority}\n\nThe diagnosis should make the remaining choice explicit. Decide only that choice. Typical lawful outcomes include:\n\n- accepting or rejecting a candidate when acceptance authority is the missing fact;\n- amending an underspecified declaration when no branch can prove a mechanical selection;\n- changing an accepted design or plan when a genuinely different revision is required;\n- releasing or replacing an authority record whose current binding is wrong; or\n- retiring a request whose objective is no longer live.\n\nDo not instruct the Driver to ignore a gate, exceed a budget, relabel a failure, or rerun identical content. Those instructions would convert authority into hidden mechanism and recreate the stall.\n\n## Resolve With an Explicit Record {#el:resolve-explicitly}\n\nWrite a resolution that:\n\n1. Names the escalation identity.\n2. States the authority decision.\n3. Pins any new or superseding artifact or declaration.\n4. Explains which failed proof the decision changes.\n5. Leaves all unaffected gates, claims, bounds, and dependency edges intact.\n\nResolution is not a mutation of the escalation's history. The escalation remains the causal record; the new authority record makes it resolved or moot under the fold.\n\n## Check Mechanical Recovery Afterward {#el:check-recovery}\n\nOn the next session, confirm one of these observable outcomes:\n\n- a prepared or open run now serves the request;\n- a declared future deferral exists;\n- a revised candidate is content-distinct from adverse predecessors;\n- the request's unmet executable conjunct set shrank; or\n- the escalation became resolved or off-path under `State.Blocks`.\n\nIf the next plan still has unmet executable conjuncts and zero prepared runs, it must append a new typed escalation or self-dedupe to the still-binding one before its plan record. The record must name the consuming branch.
