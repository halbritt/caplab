## Inferences, rivals, assumptions, and uncertainty

**Inference:** The narrow card is faithful to what C9 manipulated and measured
because it names the complete append package and exact task envelope rather
than promoting the observed contrast into a general judgment or Doctrine
claim.

Credible rivals remain literal procedure following, prompt length, imperative
salience, added attention, work-note wording, the base task's `DECISION.md`
affordance, and stochastic behavior in one task instance. The existing data do
not identify mediation or show that the subject read, understood, or reasoned
from the ledger response. The card's task-family and broader promotion gates
remain prospective, not satisfied.

The word `accept` in the owner's instruction could denote several repository
assertion types in isolation. In this context CAPLAB-20 presents exactly
`select`, `revise`, or `decline`, while CAPLAB-v0 acceptance is separately owned
by P14 after independent verification. The bounded interpretation is therefore
card selection at P2.

## Recommendation and alternatives

**Recommendation considered:** select the exact card without substantive
revision. It preserves the demonstrated exact-V treatment result and prevents
stronger engineering-judgment or Doctrine hypotheses from being treated as
established.

Alternatives were to revise the construct to evidence-responsive engineering
judgment after human artifact review and replication, narrow it to literal
instruction following, or decline a capability card and retain C9 only as a
prompt-effect experiment. The owner selected the recommendation.

`8c910c50923340d3586e82ac29fee4614eb72bfefd2347180803e1792b08fad5`.

**Owner and authority:** repository owner under repository ownership. The owner
selected the card through the direct instruction `now accept` on 2026-07-15 in

**Rationale:** the card is the narrowest contract that retains C9's controlled
