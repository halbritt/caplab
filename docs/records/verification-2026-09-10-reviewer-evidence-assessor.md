## Findings from content inspection

The optional-RSS finding, its two duplicate occurrences, the natural
partial-refresh finding and its withdrawn variant each cite the exact patch
for causal attribution. The patch shows the newly introduced provider gate or
partial-failure handler. The checker instead requires a current-code filename
prefix, so it rejects evidence that establishes the intended source contrast.
Independent observations and original requirements support both bounded
defects. Changing a citation's valid representation should not change that
conclusion.

The trace-retention and HTTP-diagnostic findings receive `mixed` where the
expectations say `unresolved`. Both combine supported source behavior with an
unresolved requirement. Our prompt's definitions permit this overlap. In the
trace finding, the assessor also identifies an overbroad every-daily-run
statement: CLI lines 505-512 return before main curation when no new candidates
exist. The trace module creates directories per successful capture. Neither
fact supplies a retention obligation or a measured long-running disk impact.
The assessor cites the packet inventory for missing evidence and requirements;
that inventory establishes only the supplied experiment coverage. Separate
primary inspection of the original README lines 217-222 finds an incident
recovery contract with no retention period. This inspection supplements the
audit; it does not repair the assessor's frozen citations.

The HTTP source contrast establishes removal of the error-body excerpt from
the error path. Calling it a "regression" must not imply an established
requirement violation. The assessor's explanation preserves the unresolved
diagnostic/privacy requirement and absence of a runtime experiment.

The natural systemd concern receives `refuted_claim` where the expectation
says `mixed`. Its pre-v250 universal predicate is contradicted by the supplied
v244 source. The assessor also preserves that v243 differs and the actual host
is unevidenced. A whole-finding refutation label loses that distinction if
used alone in scoring. The exactly specified synthetic v244 rejection claim
does have a directly refuted predicate. The original natural finding remains
uncertain and advisory; neither refutation creates an incorrect blocker.

All eleven complete explanations and their cited source/observation spans
were inspected. The reversed-RSS variant is contradicted by the constructor
condition and healthy true-flag harvest/pool controls. Keyword-only text
contains no defect proposition. Duplicates retain their original occurrences,
and withdrawal does not change the empirical truth of the partial-refresh
claim. This is primary-agent development analysis under ADR 0026, not an
independent verdict on its own experiment or scorer acceptance.
