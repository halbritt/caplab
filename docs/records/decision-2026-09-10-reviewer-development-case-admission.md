## Basis and attribution

The scheduler's required completeness comes from D0008.C1/C11, which predates
the sampled change. Original production paths demonstrate the new accepted
state, and removing the omitted observation changes the original scheduler's
placement. Ordinary, expired and supervised-fallback controls distinguish a
causal input from an unused field. The omission itself is inherited; the
admitted defect is the patch's newly accepted incomplete decision. The graph
still retains the original observation. Count one failure family regardless
of the number of revisions, conditions or repetitions.

The effort control combines original public-parser observations with a
separately implemented compiler check. The corrected batch preserves all 135
tested outcomes per revision, including 44 accepted inputs and 91 effort-field
rejections. The malformed first batch remains failed: equal revision outcomes
did not excuse its 42 failed positive controls. Expected categories describe
existing parser behavior and do not establish ideal product choices. Admit
only the two stated preservation properties; do not infer a globally clean
patch or refute an unrelated finding from test success.

The witness author inspected source and changes. Expected behavior rests on
the predating requirement or the stated preservation property, not on a
reviewer's output, a repair title or model consensus. The compiler and the
scheduler's original graph/counterfactual observations supply distinct checks;
separate agent contexts would not establish independence by themselves.

## Versioned use and reopening

The [case records](../product/studies/reviewer-ranking-001/development-case-admission-001.json)
bind full base/change/tree identities, registered archive hashes, admitted
properties, exclusions and a `case_truth_id`. Each truth ID is SHA-256 of the
canonical JSON case body with the `case_truth_id` field omitted.

These cases are development-exposed and ineligible for held-out evidence.
They are not a representative or matched comparative sample. They do not
authorize live trials or pass scorer/pilot acceptance. The fixed 32-case
feasibility sample remains unchanged: 13 have bounded behavioral witnesses,
two have only base-tree evidence and 17 remain without a change-specific
witness. Two now have the bounded development admission recorded here.

Known truth remains incomplete on both cases. Any new substantiated defect
creates a superseding truth version applied to every binding assessed on that
case. Preserve prior versions, unresolved claims and original outputs. Do not
selectively update a favored reviewer's denominator or count an unknown claim
as false. A finding against the effort control must be evaluated on its own
evidence; it can reopen the control's properties.

The next use is validation of outcome scoring and task administration across
these cases. Comparative admission still requires accepted scoring, native
bindings and pilot evidence, followed by frozen assignments, a sample-size
rationale, uncertainty/missingness rules and unused cases. This decision does
not accept a reviewer ranking or complete the active goal.
