# Reported claims and independently verified outcomes

Under ADR 0026, authorize a second development interpretation experiment in
private custody `reviewer-ranking-001/development/claim-interpretation-2`.
Preserve the first experiment and its unmet criterion unchanged. Copy its
hash-checked input documents and expected reported stances into the new
custody; do not reinterpret or replace its old result. Add six prospectively
authored review variants before execution, with frozen expectations. These
are scorer-development inputs, not actual reviewer performance samples.

The revised protocol extracts reported stances only. A paraphrase can express
a reported claim; a logically inferred rejection of a different hypothesis
is not an additional reported stance. Retain full document context by its
input hash and document ID, rather than treating a selected quotation as proof
of meaning. Unknown assertions and review limitations remain distinguishable.

Authorize one new Codex CLI 0.153.4 `codex-terra-max` native invocation, with
a 600-second process limit and one 10-second version preflight. Apply the
same native-policy enforcement, isolated task mount, disabled user rules,
configuration/apps/plugins/skill search, sealed external access-token
projection and credential-private-text/v3 capture as the first experiment.
No refresh token, answer key, source repository or empirical truth records
enter the subject task. The authorized provider receives only the frozen
review texts, neutral hypotheses and interpretation instructions. Stop on
input drift, unavailable credential, quarantine or deadline. Do not replay
a consumed slot. Expiry: consumption or 2026-09-11T00:00:00Z.

Authorize a local development assessor that connects these interpretations
to the existing timeout witness, source lineage, and topic-credential witness
by their original hashes and commit identities. Read their preserved records
without executing or rewriting historical source. Keep introduced defects,
inherited behavior, refuted concrete scenarios, missing causal attribution,
uncertainty and unknown claims distinct. Do not infer a whole-patch clean
label from a repair control or a failed attempt from an empty finding list.

Freeze the assessment criteria before running either interpretation or
assessment. Synthetic reports cannot establish native reviewer performance;
the actual prior native review remains an unscored development probe.
Neither this experiment nor matching its expectations accepts a production
scorer, case corpus, comparative study or ranking. Broader semantic validity,
coverage and independent new-finding investigation remain required.
