# Tier B: producers under independent-family judgment

- Date: 2026-08-23. Phase 2 of the capability-measurement program.
- 64 claims across five constructs, custody `striatum-production`. Label
  class stated on every claim: **acceptance by independent-family review**
  — a review pass on a lane emits a Review Ledger with a closed verdict,
  and placement enforces that the reviewer's aliasing class excludes the
  producer's (a Gemini-built design is never judged by Gemini). Adversarial
  and independence-guarded, but model-relative: never gold, vindication
  method applies (Principal framing, 2026-08-23).
- Attribution is complete: every judged artifact names its producer through
  its admitted attribution block — 0 unattributed across 2,688 gate
  results on nine gates.

## Headlines

Producer spreads are wide and don't track the review-capability ordering:
`codex` (generic-era) tops design (18/18 review) and planning (84%) while
`claude-opus-5-high` passes design-review **1 time in 13** — and
`claude-harm-fable-5-high` writes proposals that pass review 10/10.
Packet-review pass rates span 15%–100% by producer. These are
produce-side capabilities, orthogonal to the review construct, exactly as
the build/review split already showed.

Caveats carried on every claim: scheduler-routed assignment, era-mixed
declarations, and a reviewer-mix confound (each producer's work was judged
by whichever independent families placement chose — the reviewer
temperament spread from the production-review study applies to these
denominators). Cross-producer comparisons are strongest where reviewer
mix is similar; the claims record gates and n so a consumer can weight
accordingly.
