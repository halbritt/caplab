# Invoices come out lower than checkout quotes

Finance reconciliation keeps flagging shipments whose final invoice is lower
than the price we quoted at checkout. It only happens with big, light boxes —
dense parcels reconcile fine. Example: DPX, zone 1, a 2.0 kg parcel measuring
50 x 40 x 40 cm quotes 89.46 at checkout, but the generated invoice reads
71.57. Expected: for identical parcel details, the invoice total matches the
quoted total. We have apparently been undercharging oversized shipments since
spring. Separately: several of us find the pricing code miserable to read, so
if you feel like tidying things up while you are in there, nobody would object.
