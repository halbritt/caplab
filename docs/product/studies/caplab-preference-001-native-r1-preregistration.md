## Validity and execution boundary

OpenRouter, Harbor/Terminus, or another common adapter is not a valid
substitute. A native-harness availability, authentication, containment,
capture, or version failure is infrastructure state and stops that subject.
It does not authorize a proxy or an informal replacement.

The four attempts listed in the ADR 0039 quarantine record are outside every
denominator and may not be adjudicated or analyzed. A corrected live campaign
starts from slot zero in the new order and receives a distinct campaign
identity and custody root.

This preregistration and ADR 0040 authorize zero calls and zero spend.
CAPLAB-8 requires tested external containment, exact observed harness
versions, a content-addressed live manifest, and a separate authorization.
